# FUT-001 — Dairy cattle: preliminary heat stress / feed-cost feasibility and originality screen

**28 October 2026 | QUESTION ONLY / SCREENED_PRELIMINARY / HOLD | No new manuscript, experiment, result or verified novelty.**

## Candidate research question
At the **farm or herd** level, how does exposure to heat stress interact with contemporaneous feed prices to change *net milk margin per cow*, after controlling for breed, lactation stage, farm management and location? This is not equivalent to asking whether temperature alone predicts national milk production.

## Direct prior art and critical boundary
- Chen, Thorup, Kudahl and Østergaard, *Journal of Dairy Science* (2024), DOI **10.3168/jds.2023-24059**, conducted a meta-analysis of **31 studies / 34 trials** on heat stress, feed intake and milk output. The effects of THI and lactation stage were already examined. Therefore any claim that this project first links heat stress and milk yield is false. Source: https://doi.org/10.3168/jds.2023-24059 and PubMed https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38101736/ .
- PubMed explicitly records a **December 2024 erratum** (DOI 10.3168/jds.2024-107-12-11803). Its corrections must be read before borrowing numerical effect sizes or modeling assumptions. No original meta-analysis coefficient is adopted here as new data.
- A 2026 review of economic impacts of dairy heat stress (DOI **10.3390/su18168210**) discusses farm-level profit losses. Thus even a simple heat-stress/profit linkage is not presumptively novel; a demonstrable *feed-price interaction with source-matched farm economics* is the only candidate differentiation, still unproven.

## Data feasibility
- FAOSTAT https://www.fao.org/faostat/en/ publicly provides country-level livestock/commodity statistics; **national output does not supply individual cows' exposure, farm feed purchases, breed, lactation or net margins**. Public availability at portal level does not establish a complete usable panel.
- Copernicus ERA5/ERA5-Land https://climate.copernicus.eu/climate-reanalysis provides historical gridded meteorological data. Exposure would require justified herd-location matching, time-zone and heat-index construction, and data-version/licensing verification. Gridded heat is not direct barn microclimate.
- A defensible study needs **lawfully accessible, timestamped herd-level milk yields, feed prices/quantities, farm costs, herd location, lactation and management**; no such matched dataset has been acquired or permission verified.
- Data protection, commercial confidentiality, animal research/ethics determinations and source licenses remain unresolved. Observational associations must not be labeled causal.

## Proposed method if gates pass
Prespecify farm/herd panel with location-linked heat exposure and feed-cost interaction; include herd and season fixed effects where identifiable, lagged thermal stress, clustered uncertainty at assignment/exposure unit, sensitivity to feed-price endogeneity, and external validation. Explicitly compare against the 2024 meta-analysis and the 2026 economic review. No analysis is performed until original data and rights are verified.

**Gate verdict:** originality NOT VERIFIED; compatible farm-level data NOT ACQUIRED; licenses/ethics NOT CLEARED; priority A as a screening candidate only. **HOLD.** Do not divert capacity from MR, AE and ZM.

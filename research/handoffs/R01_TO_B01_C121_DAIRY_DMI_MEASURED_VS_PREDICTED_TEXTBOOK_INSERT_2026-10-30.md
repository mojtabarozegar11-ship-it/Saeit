# R-01 → B-01 | Dairy management textbook insert: measured versus predicted dry-matter intake
**Owner receiving:** B-01 (books). **Status:** READY_FOR_REVIEW as original educational copy; not inserted or published. **Date:** 2026-10-30.

## Suggested teaching text
Feed offered is not the same quantity as feed consumed. When feed and refusals are weighed on an as-fed basis, convert each to dry matter using the *measured fraction* of dry matter, then subtract refused dry matter:

**Pen dry-matter intake (kg/day) = feed offered (kg as-fed/day) × offered DM fraction − refusals (kg as-fed/day) × refusal DM fraction.**

Do not apply the offered-feed moisture percentage to refusals if their moisture differs. A pen total cannot be attributed to an individual cow without an individual intake measurement. For per-animal-time estimates, use measured pen cow-days; if feed is shared across pens without a reliable allocation, label the pen-level intake missing rather than distributing it by headcount. Record scales, moisture sampling, cow entries/exits, refusals and missing-data reasons.

**Clearly hypothetical classroom exercise (not farm observations):** A pen receives 1,000 kg feed at 45% DM and has 100 kg refusals at 30% DM. Calculated DMI = 1,000×0.45 − 100×0.30 = **420 kg DM**. If the pen contributed 40 cow-days during that 24-hour window, estimated DMI is **10.5 kg per cow-day**. This does not establish adequacy of the ration, milk yield, animal health or individual cow intake. The chosen numbers are arithmetic examples only, not nutritional targets.

### Learning checks with answers
1. Why not multiply all refusals by 45%? **Answer:** the refusals' independently measured dry-matter fraction is 30%; using 45% would misstate consumption.
2. Is 10.5 kg per cow-day an observed individual cow's intake? **Answer:** no, it is a pen aggregate divided by exposure time.
3. Does a NASEM-predicted DMI equal observed consumption? **Answer:** no; predictive equations require inputs and validation, and their output is a model estimate.

### Primary scientific anchor
National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine. *Nutrient Requirements of Dairy Cattle*, 8th revised ed., 2021, Chapter 2 “Dry Matter Intake.” Open primary book record: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK600603/ ; official NASEM source chapter table record: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK600587/ .

## Reader-ready insert (for B to review, not automatically integrated)
“Fresh cultivated mushrooms are perishable. For consumer handling in Canada, Health Canada advises keeping loose mushrooms refrigerated in a paper bag and prepackaged mushrooms in their original packaging or a paper bag, with the latter described as suitable for up to five days under its guidance. An airtight, unrefrigerated package can create conditions conducive to harmful bacterial growth; this is a safety warning, not a validated universal shelf-life model. Refrigeration slows deterioration but does not sterilize food. Do not infer that all mushroom species, modified-atmosphere packages, industrial distribution conditions or jurisdictions share the same shelf life. Follow validated product-specific packaging, time–temperature and sanitation controls.”

**Original primary source:** Amini, Emami & Dehghanisanij (2025), *Scientific Reports* 15, 42480. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-26567-z . Original Tables 4–5. B-01 alone decides on book integration; no public release approved.

## Worked case: observed water-input productivity versus inconsistent reported percentages
Original Table 4 displays F7 apple-basin orchard water productivity **4.6 (DSS section)** versus **6.0 kg/m³ (control)**, and gross applied irrigation depths **1057.6** versus **764.9 mm**. F5 apple-drip WP is **8.4** versus **4.6 kg/m³**. Table 5's column labeled “Increase in water productivity (%)” prints **−3.22** for F7 and **+3.87** for F5.

**Exercise 1 — water and WP contrast.** F7 applied irrigation difference = 1057.6−764.9 = **+292.7 mm**; displayed WP difference = 4.6−6.0 = **−1.4 kg/m³**.

**Exercise 2 — relative percentage.** F7 conventional relative WP change = (4.6/6.0−1)×100 = **−23.33%**; F5 = (8.4/4.6−1)×100 = **+82.61%**. Neither equals Table 5's printed percentage-column value; nearest-tenth rounding does not explain the discrepancies. Do not silently “correct” the publisher's values or assign an undocumented alternative definition. Original unrounded data/erratum are needed for exact adjudication.

**Exercise 3 — scope of inference.** The paper's WP equation is yield divided by **applied irrigation water**, not measured crop evapotranspiration. These are nonrandomized within-farm comparisons, not proof of basin-scale water savings or sustained farmer adoption. Treat all original numbers as reported, with an explicit source-discrepancy note. Do not reproduce publisher figures without checking rights.

# R-01 → B-01 C104: FDA food traceability, 2026 enforcement and mushroom chapter correction

**Owner:** R-01 (source adjudication). **Recipient:** B-01 (book integration only). **Stage:** FILE READY_FOR_REVIEW; no legal clearance, public edit or sale. **Date:** 2026-10-29.

## Original regulator sources checked

The [FDA Food Traceability Rule landing page](https://www.fda.gov/food/food-safety-modernization-act-fsma/fsma-final-rule-requirements-additional-traceability-records-certain-foods) states the original compliance date was **January 20, 2026**, FDA proposed **July 20, 2028**, and a 2026 Congressional directive instructed FDA **not to enforce before July 20, 2028**. Distinguish *proposed extension* from *statutory non-enforcement instruction*: neither repeals the rule's product-specific requirements. The page's October 2026 notice of a **draft** small retail/restaurant enforcement-discretion policy is **not** a blanket mushroom-producer exemption and is not yet a finalized policy.

The [FDA FTL FAQ](https://www.fda.gov/food/food-safety-modernization-act-fsma/frequently-asked-questions-fsma-food-traceability-rule) states that changing a listed fresh food to frozen/dried form can remove it from the fresh-form list, while handling the fresh listed food before transformation may still trigger receiving/other records. The chapter must classify **product form, actor, transaction and event timing**, not just 'mushrooms' as a commodity. Do not claim all mushrooms are FTL-listed or all mushroom products exempt; confirm exact FTL classification and operation with regulator text and specialist review.

**Ready-to-integrate correction for Chapter 22:** “As of 29 October 2026, FDA describes a proposed compliance-date extension to 20 July 2028 and a separate congressional instruction against enforcement before that date. These do not establish that all mushroom products or businesses are exempt. Coverage depends on the listed food and its form, the actor and transaction, any applicable exemptions, and the relevant critical tracking event. The proposed small-retailer/restaurant discretion is not a final general exemption.”

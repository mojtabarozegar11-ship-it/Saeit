## Verified source hierarchy
1. FDA final guidance **issued August 2026**, [Guide to Minimize Biological Hazards in Ready-to-Eat Fresh-Cut Produce](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/guidance-industry-guide-minimize-biological-hazards-ready-eat-fresh-cut-produce), Docket FDA-2018-D-3583. It **replaces** the October 2018 draft and the 2008 guide. [FDA announcement, 11 August 2026](https://www.fda.gov/food/hfp-constituent-updates/fda-finalizes-guidance-ready-eat-fresh-cut-produce-operations-under-preventive-controls-human-food).
2. Its specified scope is **RTE fresh-cut produce with water activity above 0.85** processed in covered manufacturing settings; it excludes uncut farm production from its intended operational scope. Processing a mushroom does not *automatically* establish that every mushroom product falls under every example or recommendation.
3. Guidance is **nonbinding unless an underlying regulation is independently applicable**; 21 CFR Part 117 applicability, exemptions and jurisdiction require product/facility-specific assessment. FDA Food Code 2022 is a **model for retail/foodservice adoption by jurisdictions**, not a blanket federal production license: [FDA Food Code](https://www.fda.gov/food/fda-food-code/food-code-2022).
## Reader-ready correction for the mushroom manuscript
> For U.S.-market ready-to-eat sliced or otherwise fresh-cut mushrooms, the facility should first document the precise product definition, whether the product is normally eaten without a pathogen-reducing step, measured water activity and the facility's regulatory status. The FDA's August 2026 final fresh-cut produce guidance can inform biological-hazard analysis and preventive-control design **where its scope fits**; it is not itself a binding rule, and its examples are not an automatic validation of a particular mushroom line. A raw whole-mushroom farm, a cooked/frozen product and a fresh-cut ready-to-eat processing line require separate applicability decisions. A qualified food-safety professional must approve the final hazard analysis, validation and records before commercialization.

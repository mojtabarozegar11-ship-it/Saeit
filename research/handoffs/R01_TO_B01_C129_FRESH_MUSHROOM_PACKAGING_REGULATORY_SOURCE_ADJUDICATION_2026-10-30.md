# R-01 → B-01 | Fresh mushroom packaging: primary-source adjudication
Date: 2026-10-30. Scope: **editorial source check only** for the mushroom textbook; no publication, operational HACCP approval or claim of regulatory authorization.

## Safe, insert-ready textbook passage (human specialist review required)
Fresh mushrooms remain biologically active after harvest. Impermeable, hermetically sealed or vacuum packaging can lower oxygen availability, and the risk of *Clostridium botulinum* toxin formation must be considered before adopting a package design. Health Canada explicitly recommends visibly perforated film for retail trays of fresh mushrooms to allow air exchange; it gives two 3.0-mm perforations as an example in its Canadian guidance, **not as a universally sufficient engineering specification**. The same guidance distinguishes hermetically sealed packages, for which refrigeration, labeling and documented shelf-life controls are important. Package selection therefore requires product-specific risk assessment, shelf-life validation, applicable local law and competent food-safety review. Visual appearance and odor cannot independently establish the absence of botulinum toxin. Never infer that vacuum sealing alone preserves fresh mushrooms safely or that a generic retail food code certifies a farm or packing plant.

## B-01 release gate
Do not copy a fixed temperature, perforation count, shelf-life, or universal HACCP exemption from one jurisdiction into a worldwide commercial instruction. B-01 must independently verify the exact textbook passage, applicable country/food category, package gas permeability, storage distribution, validation data, and competent specialist signoff.

# R-01 → B-01 | Fresh mushroom postharvest handling: source-verified book insert
Date: 2026-10-30. Owner of publication: B-01. Status: editorial handoff only, not inserted into the book or released.

## Reader-ready technical passage (English)
Fresh mushrooms are highly perishable. Cooling promptly after harvest and maintaining an uninterrupted cold chain slow quality deterioration, moisture loss and bacterial spoilage; these practices do not sterilize the product. The University of New Hampshire Extension's specialty-mushroom guidance, citing USDA Agriculture Handbook 66, reports an optimum near 32–34°F (approximately 0–1.1°C) and about 95% relative humidity for mushrooms, with a **conditional** 7–9-day quality shelf-life estimate under those conditions. It also reports that storage at 36°F (approximately 2.2°C) can reduce quality shelf life to 3–5 days. These are source-reported quality estimates, **not** validated safety expiration dates for every mushroom species, package or supply chain. The FDA's general home refrigeration recommendation is at or below 40°F (4°C); this general food-safety ceiling must not be confused with the narrower mushroom-quality optimum. Avoid condensation, monitor the product temperature and packaging, maintain traceable harvest/lot records, and reject visibly spoiled product. A market-specific HACCP assessment and local food rules remain necessary before commercial shelf-life claims.

## Educational exercise with answer
**Question:** Two otherwise comparable lots are stored at 33°F and 36°F. Is it justified to assert a universal 4-day safety extension for the colder lot? **Answer:** No. The extension source gives quality shelf-life *ranges* under specified conditions; they do not establish a controlled causal experiment, a universal species-independent safety endpoint, or an exact extension for these particular lots. A trial would require documented species, harvest maturity, package permeability, product temperature, humidity, microbial and sensory criteria, and lot-level replication.

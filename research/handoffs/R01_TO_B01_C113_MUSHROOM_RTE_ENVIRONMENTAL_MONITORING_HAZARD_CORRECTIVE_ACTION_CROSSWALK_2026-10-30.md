## Insert-ready scientific paragraph (editorial; not a site-specific compliance determination)

Where a facility's documented hazard analysis identifies environmental-pathogen contamination of exposed ready-to-eat food as a hazard requiring a preventive control, environmental monitoring is one component of verifying the control's effectiveness. The monitoring program should define scientifically justified sampling sites, organism or indicator, sampling schedule, analytical method, laboratory, and corrective-action pathway. A positive result must be assessed in relation to the food, process, location and affected-lot exposure history; a negative environmental result alone does not certify every food unit or justify automatic release. Facility-specific legal applicability, qualified preventive-controls review, product testing and documented corrective actions remain separate decisions. This discussion does not prescribe a universal sampling frequency or claim that all raw cultivated mushrooms are ready-to-eat.

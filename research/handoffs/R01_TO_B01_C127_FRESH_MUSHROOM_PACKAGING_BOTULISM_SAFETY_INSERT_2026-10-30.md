# R-01 → B-01 | Fresh-mushroom packaging hazard: source-checked editorial insert
Date: 2026-10-30. Recipient owner B-01. **Editorial handoff, not a released chapter, certification or validated shelf-life protocol.**

## Ready-to-integrate textbook section (English)
**Fresh mushroom packaging requires both temperature control and an appropriate oxygen-transfer design.** Harvested mushrooms continue to respire. In an insufficiently ventilated sealed package, their respiration can lower oxygen levels, creating conditions relevant to *Clostridium botulinum* toxin formation. The hazard cannot be ruled out merely because a package looks intact or the mushrooms show no obvious spoilage. Therefore, packaging selection is a food-safety control, not only a cosmetic or moisture-retention decision.

The University of New Hampshire Extension advises against airtight packaging and suggests breathable options such as paper bags or ventilated containers for specialty mushrooms. Health Canada's fresh-mushroom guidance recommends visibly perforated plastic overwrap to allow air exchange and discusses a specific perforation example; **that example is not a universal design specification** for every mushroom species, tray, film, temperature or market. The Canadian guidance also discusses separate obligations for hermetically sealed fresh mushrooms, including refrigeration at 4°C or below and required labelling. The applicability of those provisions depends on product and jurisdiction; a producer must confirm current local rules before use.

In the United States, 21 CFR 112.115 requires covered produce to be packaged to prevent *C. botulinum* toxin formation when the hazard is known or reasonably foreseeable, expressly naming mushrooms as an example. No generic permeability, perforation count, nominal refrigeration temperature or visual inspection can substitute for a validated, product-specific hazard assessment. Cold-chain quality shelf-life estimates must never be represented as microbiologically validated safety expiration dates.

**Production decision:** Before specifying a commercial package, document mushroom species, respiration rate, film gas permeability, package dimensions, storage temperature/time distribution, product form, intended market, and validation of the relevant microbial hazard controls. Use a qualified food-safety specialist to review the plan. Do not assign a guaranteed shelf life without appropriate evidence.

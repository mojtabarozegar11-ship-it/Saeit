## Source-qualified correction for the packaging chapter

Health Canada's original public [food-safety tips for mushrooms](https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/food-safety-fruits-vegetables/mushrooms.html) warns that fresh mushrooms in unrefrigerated airtight packaging may support growth of organisms associated with botulism. It advises consumers to keep loose mushrooms in a paper bag in the refrigerator, or prepackaged mushrooms refrigerated in original packaging, and to choose packages with air-permeable perforated film. These are **consumer safety recommendations**; they are not proof that every package design is validated, nor a shelf-life study for B's proposed commercial packaging.

The [FDA FSMA Food Traceability Rule overview](https://www.fda.gov/food/food-safety-modernization-act-fsma/fsma-final-rule-requirements-additional-traceability-records-certain-foods) requires additional CTE/KDE records **only for foods on the Food Traceability List and covered operations**, subject to exemptions. The FDA states it will not enforce the rule before **20 July 2028** under the 2026 congressional directive. **Do not** state that all fresh mushrooms automatically fall on the FTL or that every mushroom business is subject to the same CTE/KDE rule; verify the exact food form, operations, jurisdiction and exemptions before legal claims. Independent lot-level traceability is still sound risk management even when a particular FSMA additional-record rule does not apply.

## Proposed B editor's insertion (not yet authorized for publication)

> **Fresh mushroom package validation is a safety decision, not a cosmetic choice.** An airtight or unperforated fresh-mushroom pack held without appropriate temperature control can create an anaerobic hazard; packaging permeability, verified cold-chain controls, product/process validation and jurisdiction-specific labeling must be reviewed together. A lot genealogy record identifies the batch, packaging revision, operator, temperature evidence and receiving/shipping custody, but a complete record **does not itself prove microbial safety**. Separate physical packaging validation, hazard analysis and authentic temperature measurements from synthetic teaching examples.

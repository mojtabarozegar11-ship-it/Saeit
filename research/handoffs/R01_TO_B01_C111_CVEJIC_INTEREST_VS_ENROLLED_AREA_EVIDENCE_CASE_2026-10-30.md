## Insert-ready case (original study, not a new field trial)
A 2020 open-access irrigation-DSS case study in Slovenia reports a regional base of 2,518 agricultural holdings and 11,337 ha. Its October 2016 call attracted 35 interested farms representing 207.15 ha; a subsequent statement identifies 38.5 ha included after participation agreements. The figures describe **different stages and units**. They correspond to about 1.4% of holdings expressing interest, 1.8% of regional land in the interest group, and 0.3% of regional land in the agreement-included area. The 38.5/207.15 area ratio (~18.6%) must not be presented as farmer retention, an irrigation saving, a water-use change, or a conversion rate of signed farms. The number of farms associated with the 38.5 ha cannot be reconstructed without a linked participant register.

## Editorial placement and verification
Suggested destination: farm-management/economics chapter on **project recruitment, adoption funnels and denominators**, not a chapter on realized water savings. Add a two-column exercise requiring students to distinguish a *holding-count fraction* from an *area fraction*; require them to mark farm-level retention and causal water savings as **not estimable**.

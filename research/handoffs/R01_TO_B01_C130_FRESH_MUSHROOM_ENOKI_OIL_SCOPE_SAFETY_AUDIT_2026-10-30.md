## New source-scope correction
A fresh whole *Agaricus* mushroom package, a raw enoki package, and mushrooms submerged in oil are **different products and risk/control contexts**. A rule established for one must not be generalized to the other two without an applicable authority and a product-specific validation.

## Paste-ready editorial replacement (pending B specialist signoff)
“Packaging recommendations must be scoped to the species, package permeability, gas exchange, temperature, storage duration and applicable jurisdiction. A perforation layout used in an illustrative exercise is **not** an independently validated safe design. Hermetically sealed fresh mushroom packages and oil-preserved mushroom products require different hazard assessments. Raw enoki-specific *Listeria* controls should not be generalized without evidence to other mushroom species. Product safety and shelf life require a documented preventive-control assessment and, where indicated, appropriate validation by a qualified food-safety professional.”

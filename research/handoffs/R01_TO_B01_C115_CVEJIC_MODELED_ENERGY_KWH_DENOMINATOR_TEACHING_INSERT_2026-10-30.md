## Reader-ready farm-management example

The irrigation decision-support study models annual energy requirements under two soil-water replenishment strategies: to field capacity or to 85% of the available reservoir (FC-WP). Table A10's absolute-energy column uses **kWh**, and Appendix C identifies **kWh**, although the Table A10 caption mistakenly labels absolute energy savings `m³`. Of 35 farm IDs, **28** have positive simulated energy savings, **3** (IDs 8, 12, 26) have zero, and **4** (22, 28, 30, 35) are missing (`/`). The source's 2017 total **28,581.55 kWh** divided by **28 positive rows** reproduces its published mean **1,020.77 kWh**. Dividing by all 35 rows instead gives **816.62 kWh**. The published conditional mean is therefore **not an all-participant mean**. Neither statistic is observed electricity-bill savings: both describe simulated scenarios, not meter-confirmed outcomes.

For a farm investment appraisal, collect meter-confirmed electricity and pumped-water volumes, pump efficiency and lift, tariffs, crop yields, weather and a credible comparison condition before estimating actual net benefit. Keep kWh (energy), m³ (water), kg CO₂ (emissions) and currency (financial outcomes) separate. Never treat `/` as a measured zero or interpret modelled percentage savings as independent causal adoption evidence.

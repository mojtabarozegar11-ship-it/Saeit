# R-01 → B-01 | Car (2012) irrigation-DSS denominator teaching case
**29 October 2026 | Independent editorial handoff | READY_FOR_REVIEW by B-01, not published**

## Where to use
Farm economics, research methodology or irrigation decision-support chapters. The lesson is about **auditing a published denominator**, not proving that SMS advice saves water. It may be used as an instructor-facing insert without copying the original paper's prose.

## Evidence and worked check
The original Car et al. (2012) printed Table 5 lists six group counts: **26, 7, 3, 6, 15, 17**. Their sum is **74**, yet the printed total is **72**. Every printed group percentage matches its count divided by **72**, to the displayed precision, so the table's *percentage arithmetic denominator* is recoverable. But the row percentages add to **103.2%**, and the nearby prose describes Groups 1–3 as **68%** although their printed counts add to **36/72 = 50%**. The prose and table also swap the behavioral descriptions of groups 4 and 5. These contradictions do not establish which row or label, if any, should be corrected. The UNE institutional abstract independently reports **45 full-season, 13 half-season and 14 never-submitting** participants out of 72, a different engagement-duration classification.

**Teaching exercise:** Students should (a) reproduce the six percentages with denominator 72; (b) calculate the category-count sum and explain why a sum above 100% prevents treating the six rows as a verified exclusive partition; (c) distinguish participation, reported decision influence and observed water-use change; (d) propose the original evidence needed before a source correction can be made.

**Answer key:** (a) 36.11%, 9.72%, 4.17%, 8.33%, 20.83%, 23.61%; (b) 74 counts and 103.2% printed; (c) SMS engagement and questionnaire perceptions are not a measured recommendation-to-irrigation-event link; (d) original coded roster, eligibility and group definitions, respondent IDs, dated actions and authenticated publisher correction. Do **not** invent a corrected Table 5 or infer irrigation-water savings.

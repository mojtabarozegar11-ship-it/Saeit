# R-01 to B-01 — Amini F7 editorial correction for irrigation / farm-economics teaching

**Date:** 2026-10-29. **Owner of adoption:** B-01. **Status:** READY_FOR_REVIEW; no B-owned files changed or published.

The original [Amini et al. (2025) publisher article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-26567-z) labels **Table 4 as irrigation depth** and **Table 5 as measured yield and water productivity**. A previous R manuscript incorrectly attributed F7 WP values to Table 4; R C102 corrects this. For F7 the paper gives **−1.3 kg/m³** in one Results passage, **−1.4 kg/m³** in the later Table 5 discussion, and **−0.1 kg/m³** in the Conclusion. Displayed one-decimal WP values **4.6 versus 6.0 kg/m³** imply **−1.4** by subtraction, but the original unrounded observations have not been obtained. The source also reports **292.7 mm greater irrigation depth** for the treatment section, not a saving.

**Recommended book edit (only after B owner checks its relevant chapter):** cite *Amini Table 5* for WP, *Table 4* for depth, describe the F7 effect as **source-inconsistent**, and teach readers to keep irrigation depth, crop yield, WP, perceived uptake and causal water savings separate. Do not select −0.1, −1.3 or −1.4 as a validated exact effect; do not quote the paper's pooled p-value as an F7-specific significance test. Source data are available only on request to corresponding author according to publisher statement.

**Acceptance gate for B:** confirm original chapter location, patch with tracked change, retain full citation DOI 10.1038/s41598-025-26567-z, verify no unsupported aggregate water-saving claim, and return a separate B-owned QC result. This handoff is not a published book correction.

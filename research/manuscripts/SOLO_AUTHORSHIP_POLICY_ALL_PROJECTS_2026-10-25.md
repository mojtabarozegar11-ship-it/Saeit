## Invariant policy
1. **Exactly one named human author is the programme's default:** Mojtaba Roozegar. No automatic invitations to coauthorship; do not claim another reviewer, coauthor, laboratory participant or institution without actual involvement.
2. **ChatGPT is a tool, NOT a coauthor, independent human screener, human editor or peer reviewer.** It may assist with searching, bibliography, source comparisons, original-language draft preparation, reproducible computing, source integrity checks and submission logistics within available permissions. Describe material generative-AI use transparently under the selected journal's policy, and retain accountable human verification.
3. **Scientific contribution and honesty first:** actual author must materially frame the research question, make scientifically significant analytical and interpretative decisions, verify key data and citations, substantially review/revise the text and explicitly accept full responsibility. A simple approval or AI-generated paper without meaningful human contribution does not establish qualifying authorship. Never assign other people's experimental results as original work by Mojtaba Roozegar.
4. **No fake independent review:** for a prospective systematic/scoping review seeking JBI-style independent dual human screening, do not describe ChatGPT as the second reviewer. If only one human is available, select a genuinely journal-permitted single-author manuscript design with truthful methods rather than fabricate dual-screening.
5. **Not all projects must be narrative reviews:** original single-author secondary analyses, methodology studies, perspectives and critical reviews may all be considered only if empirically valid and appropriate for a verified journal. Original external GEO reanalysis demands actual downloaded datasets, reproducible analysis, originality and legitimate data reuse before reporting new results.
6. **Authorship restriction is conditional on ethics:** if any person genuinely makes an authorship-qualifying contribution, do not omit that contributor merely to enforce a single-author label. Obtain a suitable contribution/licensing resolution with the person's consent; if a single-author paper would otherwise misattribute credit, change the project scope, remove that contribution only where scientifically/ethically permissible, or request author approval to reconsider. A blanket preference is not permission to misattribute.
7. **Publication integrity:** verify legal/professional byline, truthful research and possible organizational affiliation, ORCID, funding, competing interests, study/data permissions, copyright, references, AI declaration, publication fees and journal fit. No journal submissions without explicit approval of final manuscript and journal/fee/disclosure terms, and official portal access. The programme's generic single-author approval is NOT final submission authorization.
8. **CV evidence:** list in preparation as unpublished; submitted/under review only with real publisher receipt and Manuscript ID; accepted/published/indexed only on primary evidence. The sole authorship policy does not generate any publication credential by itself.

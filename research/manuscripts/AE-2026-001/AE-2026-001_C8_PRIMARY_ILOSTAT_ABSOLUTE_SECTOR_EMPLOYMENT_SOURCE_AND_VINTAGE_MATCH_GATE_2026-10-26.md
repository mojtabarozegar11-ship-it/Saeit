# AE-2026-001 C8 — official ILOSTAT absolute agricultural employment: source-eligibility and vintage-match gate

**Date:** 26 October 2026. **Assessment:** Internal AI source and methods review, not human peer review. **Decision:** a primary-source *absolute employment* dataset family is identified, but independent denominator validation is **NOT COMPLETE**; article remains MAJOR REVISION / NO SUBMISSION.

## New primary-source finding

The ILOSTAT official data catalogue, https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/ (read 26 October 2026), explicitly lists **“Employment by sex and economic activity (thousands)” — Annual**, as well as the annual **“Employment by sex and economic activity - ISIC level 2 (thousands)”** series. These are absolute numbers in *thousands of persons*, unlike the World Bank/ILO indicator `SL.AGR.EMPL.ZS`, which is the **percentage of total employment in agriculture**. The source family therefore passes the **unit-type eligibility** test for an independent agriculture employment denominator, but **no matching ILOSTAT CSV, release-vintage archive, or country-year values were downloaded or reconciled in this review**. The catalogue's existence is not evidence that all 155 economies or both 2000/2019 endpoints are available, or that its values match the WDI denominator.

## Required exact comparability protocol before any empirical validation claim

1. Download the official annual ILOSTAT **employment by sex and economic activity (thousands)** file, recording the *actual* downloadable URL, retrieval timestamp, bytes, SHA256, indicator code, catalogue metadata and release/version date. Do not invent an indicator code from search snippets.
2. Retain total sex, agricultural economic activity (ISIC section A where supported), total employed persons, annual frequency, and a single consistent classification; record which classification revision is used and whether it covers agriculture, forestry and fishing.
3. Confirm country-code crosswalk, national territory, observation year 2000 and 2019, employment concept, sex/age coverage, survey versus modeled source status, time-series breaks and missingness. Do not substitute employment *share* or labor force counts for agricultural workers.
4. Match **2024 and 2025 historical releases** to the corresponding WDI archives *if available*. A current ILOSTAT download, even with 2000/2019 historical years, is **not** a historical 2024/2025 vintage. If matching releases cannot be recovered, explicitly label any comparison **cross-source level plausibility at the current release**, not historical revision attribution.
5. Compute the WDI algebraically implied `L* = NV.AGR.TOTL.KD / NV.AGR.EMPL.KD` in **persons**, compare against ILOSTAT thousands after multiplying by 1000, report coverage and residual distribution by economy/year/vintage, and avoid interpreting the identity as independent evidence. Source metadata may differ even when units match.
6. Apply the same 155-economy intersection only to complete, truly comparable observations; disclose attrition and avoid filling missing values or treating a cross-sectional source agreement as proof of causation.

## Scientific disposition

The previously reported 155-economy decomposition and 40/155 comparison remain an **algebraic identity**, not an ILOSTAT-validated employment mechanism. The independent source family is now concretely identified and the validation pathway is specified; the missing release-aligned dataset is still a publication blocker for claims about independently observed employment revisions. No new quantitative employment result, manuscript novelty, causal conclusion, or submission is asserted.

## 26 October source-code and classification-revision correction (C8a)

**New source-specific evidence:** the ILOSTAT *official country-profile* catalogue links employment-by-economic-activity records to the dataset identifier `EMP_TEMP_SEX_ECO_NB_A`, e.g. its Albania indicator record: https://ilostat.ilo.org/data/country-profiles/?ref_area=ALB . This identifies an official candidate dataset code, **not** a verified downloaded file, row count or historical release. The ILO-maintained Rilostat documentation (https://ilostat.github.io/Rilostat/articles/Rilostat.html) describes the indicator-frequency convention and bulk download access. The actual URL, file bytes, release date, and checksum still must be captured at retrieval.

**Critical economic-activity harmonisation correction:** the ILOSTAT official broad-sector concordance explicitly maps **agriculture** to **ISIC Rev. 4 section A**, **ISIC Rev. 3 sections A AND B**, and **ISIC Rev. 2 major division 1** (https://ilostat.ilo.org/methods/concepts-and-definitions/classification-economic-activities/). Thus a filter that blindly selects `A` for every ISIC revision will **under-count Rev. 3 agriculture** by omitting fishing (section B). The WDI numerator covers *agriculture, forestry and fishing*; denominator validation must therefore retain the correct full sector according to the source's *actual* classification revision and activity coding. **Never aggregate across incompatible ISIC revisions or combine their overlapping total/partial categories.** For Rev. 3, aggregate disjoint A+B records *only* when the source offers both as separate components for the same country/year/source/sex; if an already aggregated agriculture category is provided, do not double-count it. Reject unlabelled, mixed-revision or ambiguously aggregated rows.

**Analytical implications:** a source-level employment comparison can only be interpreted as a plausibility audit of the WDI inferred denominator if the country, time, ISIC revision, sex, source, units and coverage agree. A contemporary ILOSTAT extract cannot explain **historical 2024→2025 WDI revisions** without independently verified historical ILOSTAT vintages. **No absolute employment observations have been fetched in this addendum; no quantitative comparison, causal finding, or publication gate is changed.**


## C8b — actual original ILOSTAT source downloaded and byte-hashed (26 October 2026)

The earlier source-family-only gate has advanced: **actual original ILOSTAT data bytes have now been downloaded**, read with native R `readRDS`, SHA256-checked and retained as a GitHub Actions artifact. The ILO-maintained [Rilostat source code](https://github.com/ilostat/Rilostat/blob/master/R/get_ilostat.R) explicitly constructs `https://rplumber.ilo.org/files/indicator/<dataset-id>.rds`; the verified source is `https://rplumber.ilo.org/files/indicator/EMP_TEMP_SEX_ECO_NB_A.rds`. **Read-only source-acquisition run:** [40299957865](https://github.com/mojtabarozegar11-ship-it/Saeit/actions/runs/40299957865), **COMPLETED/SUCCESS**; [source/endpoint artifact 12639259429](https://github.com/mojtabarozegar11-ship-it/Saeit/actions/runs/40299957865/artifacts/12639259429). Source RDS bytes: **2,306,384**; SHA256: `956f6d27cbf9a6cba969cf7ceb37b1715b40704c984fdcc76ea6aff2a48ae0cb`.

The actual RDS has **617,241** rows across years, sexes, economic-activity classes and sources; a *raw, unfiltered* 2000/2019 extract contains **40,947** rows spanning **257 distinct REF_AREA codes**. These 257 codes are **not** 257 eligible WDI economies or 257 independently observed agricultural-employment country pairs: the extract includes multiple sexes, activity classes, sources and potentially regional aggregates. The actual columns are `ref_area|source|indicator|sex|classif1|time|obs_value|obs_status|note_classif|note_indicator|note_source|best_source`. Sex codes include `SEX_T` and economic-activity codes include **`ECO_AGGREGATE_AGR`**, **`ECO_ISIC3_A`**, **`ECO_ISIC3_B`**, **`ECO_ISIC4_A`**; best-source flag is `1/0`. These original-source facts now permit a source-locked, explicitly selected total-sex, broad-agriculture, best-source coverage audit without mixing Rev.3 and Rev.4 component codes.

**Remaining gate:** do not treat the raw 40,947 rows or 257 REF_AREA codes as a final worker-count panel. The separate `ECO_AGGREGATE_AGR` / `SEX_T` / `best_source=1` extraction must be run, duplicates and survey-source changes audited, countries intersected with the fixed WDI cohort, and an independently sourced *value-added numerator* obtained before comparing the WDI inferred `L*=V/P` to actual ILOSTAT absolute counts. This is a **current-release source**, not an archived 2024 or 2025 ILOSTAT vintage. No causal attribution, 155-economy independent denominator validation, publication novelty or submission is asserted.

All counts are actual original-source analysis outputs, **not** assumed coverage of the 155-economy historical-vintage WDI cohort. Note that ILOSTAT REF_AREA includes country/territory/aggregate codes; the 73 exact WDI ISO3 matches are the relevant initial screening intersection, not a final harmonised 155-economy denominator validation.

The source-level results are **genuine independently observed/modelled ILOSTAT absolute employment counts in thousands**, but source-code stability does not prove matching survey populations, employment definitions or historical release vintages. A further analysis must independently fetch official WDI `NV.AGR.TOTL.KD` and compare its `L*=V/P` to ILOSTAT absolute persons at matched country-year endpoints, reporting residuals and source differences. **No such WDI worker-denominator comparison is claimed completed by this C8c audit**, and no causal attribution of 2024→2025 revisions is permissible.

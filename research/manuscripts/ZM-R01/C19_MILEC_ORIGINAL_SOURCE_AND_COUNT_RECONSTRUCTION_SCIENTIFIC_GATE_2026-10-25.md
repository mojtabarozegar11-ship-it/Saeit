## 3. A more specific research question
**Does elevated CO2 modify the non-additive drought-by-heat transcriptomic response in Alana wheat leaf at GS61, after explicit modeling of all lower-order effects?** The appropriate count-level factorial design is `~ D * H * C`, with eight groups and three biological replicates each (24 libraries, full rank eight coefficients, residual df 16). The coefficient of interest is the **D:H:C** interaction. This question is **a hypothesis requiring novelty comparison**, not a demonstrated unique mechanism or claim that the original study did not analyze it.

For each gene, a descriptive interaction on mean log2(FPKM+1) can be written as:
`(CTD - CD - CT + eC) - (TD - D - eT + CTR)`, where each symbol denotes the corresponding condition mean. This is a difference of two drought-by-heat interactions, one under elevated CO2 and one at ambient CO2. **It is NOT a raw-count log2 fold-change coefficient, significance test or causal mechanism.**

### Additional direct prior-art constraint: three-factor wheat physiology predates this reanalysis

Li, Kristiansen, Rosenqvist and Liu (2019), *Journal of Agronomy and Crop Science*, DOI [10.1111/jac.12330](https://doi.org/10.1111/jac.12330), studied **ambient versus elevated CO₂ combined with drought and heat in two wheat cultivars (Gladius and Paragon)** and reported physiological, ABA, water-status and yield responses. The publisher abstract already frames elevated CO₂ as a modifier of drought/heat effects. Therefore, the broad biological question “Does CO₂ alter wheat drought–heat responses?” **is not novel**. A defensible ZM-R01 contribution would have to be much narrower: a statistically validated **gene-level D:H:C interaction in Alana leaf at GS61**, with explicit comparison to Milec (2025), other transcriptomic studies and Li (2019). Li's reported phenotypes and cultivars are **not** equivalent to a 24-library Alana RNA-seq interaction coefficient; do not claim that Li already measured the proposed coefficient, or that a different molecular estimand is necessarily novel. This is a new original-publisher prior-art check, **not** new count data, FDR or biological discovery.

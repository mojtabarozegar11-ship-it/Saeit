# ZM-R01 C34 — Original IWGSC RefSeq v2.0 assembly versus annotation lineage

**27 October 2026.** New primary-source provenance check of IWGSC/URGI release history against Milec et al. (2025) published Methods. **No genome download, FASTQ download, checksum verification, RNA read counts or DESeq2 run.**

## Newly verified official release-history facts
1. The International Wheat Genome Sequencing Consortium (IWGSC) officially released **RefSeq assembly v2.0 in July 2019** under a **Toronto pre-publication access agreement**. Its contemporaneous announcement states that the assembly **had not yet been annotated**, and a v2.0 annotation was **planned** for 2020; a planned release is **not proof of a particular available gene model**. Primary: https://www.wheatgenome.org/resources/sequences/iwgsc-refseq-v2.0-now-available-at-urgi ; contemporaneous newsletter https://www.wheatgenome.org/news/newsletters/iwgsc-newsletter-3-2019 .
2. The original URGI **v2.0 README** explicitly says the assembly was pre-annotation and that MD5s accompany data; it documents `iwgsc_refseqv2.0_chrUn.fa.zip` and `iwgsc_refseqv2.0_unplaced_scaffolds.fa.zip` as alternative representations of unplaced sequences. Primary: https://urgi.versailles.inrae.fr/download/iwgsc/IWGSC_RefSeq_Assemblies/v2.0/iwgsc_refseqv2.0_README.pdf . Do not concatenate both unplaced representations into one reference.
3. The **distinct v2.1** README states that v2.1 further refined v2.0 using CDS comparisons with annotation v1.1, noting **9,416 genes** with mismatch/SNP/one-base indel concerns among **269,428** genes and replacement of problematic regions with corresponding v1.0 contigs. This is the official **v2.1 assembly revision history**, not a count of affected genes in the Milec data and not proof that the Milec authors used v2.1. Primary: https://urgi.versailles.inrae.fr/download/iwgsc/IWGSC_RefSeq_Assemblies/v2.1/iwgsc_refseqv2.1_assembly_README.pdf .
4. The published Milec paper describes alignment to **“RefSeq v2.0, EnsemblPlants”** but does **not identify** the exact genome FASTA accession, gene annotation release, transcript-to-gene mapping, GTF/GFF, checksum or multimapper handling in the inspected text (see C33). The word “EnsemblPlants” alone does not establish that its annotation coordinates correspond to the stated v2.0 assembly.

## Consequence for reproducibility
The original v2.0 assembly and a possibly later EnsemblPlants gene model **cannot be assumed to be a coordinate-compatible pair** without a manifest and sequence/annotation coordinate validation. Likewise v2.1 is **not** a drop-in byte-identical replacement for v2.0. In hexaploid wheat, reference-version mismatch and duplicated unplaced scaffolds can influence assignment of multi-mapping fragments; this is a **technical risk**, not a proven error in the published paper.

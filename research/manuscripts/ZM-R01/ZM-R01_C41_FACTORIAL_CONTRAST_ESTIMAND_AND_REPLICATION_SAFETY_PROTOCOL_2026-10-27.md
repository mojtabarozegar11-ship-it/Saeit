- [C41 contrast coefficient table](ZM-R01_C41_PRESPECIFIED_EIGHT_CELL_FACTORIAL_CONTRAST_WEIGHTS_2026-10-27.tsv), 8 rows and 7 scientific contrasts. Its columns each sum to **zero** exactly. All eight cells have explicit weights. This is an estimand specification, **not a fitted model**.

Let m(d,h,c) be a **hypothetical cell-specific log expected gene count** at binary D,H,C, after appropriate offset/normalization and model assumptions. No observed m is available. Define:
- D_avg = 1/4 × sum over h,c [m(1,h,c) − m(0,h,c)].
- H_avg and C_avg analogously, each averaging four simple effects.
- DH_avg = 1/2 × sum over c [m(1,1,c) − m(1,0,c) − m(0,1,c) + m(0,0,c)].
- DC_avg and HC_avg analogously.
- DHC = [m(1,1,1) − m(1,0,1) − m(0,1,1) + m(0,0,1)] − [m(1,1,0) − m(1,0,0) − m(0,1,0) + m(0,0,0)].

The TSV uses exact rational weights in decimal notation: main ±0.25, two-way ±0.5, three-way ±1. These define seven **cell-log-mean contrasts**; they are not the treatment-coded DESeq2 coefficients for a model `~ D*H*C`. In particular, treatment-coded D coefficient represents the D contrast at H=0,C=0, not D_avg. A contrast of log expected counts is not identical to the log of an arithmetic mean of expected counts. Model fitting and the appropriate coefficient-to-cell transformation must be verified before use.

# MR-2026-001 C59 — Claim eligibility for eight illustrative cases: G/D/R/A chain remains unverified

**28 October 2026 | Internal critical synthesis of C58, not a new source search or new field study | MAJOR REVISION / NO SUBMISSION**

## Exact scope and decision rule

Input: the **existing** [eight-row, nine-column C58 extraction](MR-2026-001_C58_EIGHT_CASE_CLAIM_UNIT_EVIDENCE_EXTRACTION_2026-10-28.tsv), which was read anew. This is an internal *documentation-sufficiency* audit, not an independent re-reading of all eight publisher full texts. It is not a systematic-review screening count.

A case may be called **complete documented event-level G/D/R/A evidence** only if the *same time- and site-matched unit* has verifiable (G) generated recommendation, (D) delivery to a specific decision maker, (R) receipt or exposure, (A) independent implemented irrigation action, and an attributable **measured** water outcome or properly identified comparator. Self-reported consideration, seasonal totals, simulated ET/yield, station-imposed treatments, and virtual decisions do not automatically satisfy this chain. Any unverified link is **NOT ESTABLISHED**, not proof of nonexistence.

**Result: 0 of 8 currently documented as complete by the C58 extraction.** This is a count of **evidence established in this limited project extraction**, not a claim that no original author collected the missing data, not a count of eight negative trials, and not a claim of priority for the proposed framework.

## Required scientific decision

The current manuscript must use **"not established in the reviewed extraction"**, never **"demonstrated absent in the literature"**. Do not pool heterogeneous percentages or compute a meta-analytic adherence rate. A new original dataset linking recommendation timestamps, delivery, receipt, action, water metering and counterfactual controls—or a newly verified source that provides these elements—is required to validate the full chain. Until then, the G/D/R/A concept is a **proposed reporting taxonomy**, not a validated outcome metric or unique research invention.

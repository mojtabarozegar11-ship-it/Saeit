## Screening decision rules (protocol proposal; may be amended before freeze)
- `INCLUDE_FOR_FULLTEXT`: source may be original real-world evaluation of actionable irrigation advice to human farmer/manager; or clearly valid contextual B/C under explicit prespecified source selection. This does **not** mean final inclusion.
- `EXCLUDE`: clearly outside agricultural irrigation decision-making or clearly not qualifying source/document type. Give the exact explicit reason (e.g., review article as primary-study candidate; unrelated nutrient-only tool; autonomous closed-loop without human advice where contextual inclusion criteria not met).
- `UNSURE`: ambiguous human recipients, unclear field deployment, missing/fragmentary abstract, unclear document type or local water-management context — retain for full text and adjudication rather than automatically exclude.
- Always preserve A/B/C stratum tags and M/O/S/P/U provenance at full-text charting. Self-reported adherence is not independently measured adoption; simulations are not realized water savings. An approved scope document governs group definitions.
- **Abstract not available**: do not assign a definitive title/abstract exclusion solely because a reliable abstract was missing; flag `UNSURE / ABSTRACT_NOT_FOUND` and retrieve a full text where feasible. Machine HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW is triage only and MUST NOT bias reviewers toward automatic selection.
## Human reviewer workflow
1. Secure TWO suitably qualified independent human reviewers (at least one domain-qualified irrigation/agricultural research specialist, preferably another systematic-review trained screener), document their real identities, expertise, roles and conflict-of-interest statements. Obtain permission before storing personal reviewer details publicly; restricted storage may be necessary.
2. Each reviewer separately reviews the *same* 25 title/abstract records with this eligibility guide without seeing the other's decisions. Use public bibliographic metadata and access links, not only automated machine-priority scores; a blinded view without the priority column is recommended.
3. Record a decision and reason per item in separate secure review copies, then timestamp and import only approved non-sensitive outputs. Calculate raw agreement = agreements / 25; diagnostic threshold >=19 agreements (75% of 25 is 18.75, round up to 19). Agreement alone does not prove screening accuracy; inspect exclusion reasons and diversity of disagreement.
4. Convene reviewers to resolve disagreements and refine criteria. Record all changes under a new version with reasons; then start systematic screening only after criteria and workflow are sufficiently clear. Do NOT backdate authorship contributions or conduct human reviews using an automated agent.
5. For all 604 current preliminary citation records, undertake genuine independent review with an audit ledger, confirm duplicate merges, source completeness, revise search sensitivity, obtain source text and log eligibility decisions. Count PRISMA flow only after validated exports and actual selection evidence.

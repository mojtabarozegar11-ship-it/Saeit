# MR-2026-001: independent 2026 prior-art and implementation-fidelity challenge

**Date:** 2026-10-26. **Decision:** previous six-link worksheet overlaps established reporting and implementation-fidelity frameworks; retain only a narrow, explicitly *unvalidated*, sector-specific proposal. This AI science audit is NOT human peer review, a new discovery, or a journal green light.

## Independently checked originals

1. [Hoffmann et al., BMJ (2014), DOI 10.1136/bmj.g1687](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.g1687): the twelve-item **TIDieR** checklist, including items 11–12 for planned and actual intervention fidelity, predates this manuscript. Cannot claim original invention of fidelity/delivery/receipt chain.
2. [Carroll et al., Implementation Science (2007), DOI 10.1186/1748-5908-2-40](https://doi.org/10.1186/1748-5908-2-40): implementation fidelity including adherence, exposure and participant responsiveness predates current proposal. Generic stage separation is not new.
3. [Al-Kilani et al., Technology in Agronomy (online 2026-10-10), DOI 10.48130/tia-0026-0009](https://doi.org/10.48130/tia-0026-0009): 45 Jordanian farms assessed by field visits/interviews, 4.4% of farms had identified scientific scheduling parameters (FC/Kc) per publisher abstract; does NOT report per-advice-event irrigation adherence. Source is open full text, but our check used its publisher abstract and source metadata.
4. [Zhu & Feng, Agricultural Systems (2026), DOI 10.1016/j.agsy.2026.104867](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.agsy.2026.104867): Q-method study with 45 smallholders in Shaanxi identifies distinct post-adoption adaptation logics and reversibility. Scope is *water-saving irrigation technology adoption*, not necessarily a farmer-facing DSS; publisher abstract verified, methods not independently reanalyzed.
5. [Springer Nature AI policy](https://group.springernature.com/gp/group/ai/ai-guidance-for-researchers-editors-reviewers): AI can assist reasoning and source analysis, but responsibility for intellectual content and authorship must remain with a human. General delegation cannot represent substantive author review or certify funding/COI/rights.

## Crosswalk: no fabricated methodological priority

| Proposed MR link | Closest established content | Narrow possible adaptation; not validated |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Advice generation | TIDieR intervention specification, dose and tailoring | Record *timestamped plot-specific irrigation advice quantity*. |
| 2. Transmission | TIDieR actual delivery | Distinguish produced from sent recommendation. |
| 3. Receipt | Carroll exposure/participant responsiveness | Per-farmer receipt evidence with source class. |
| 4. Irrigation event | TIDieR actual fidelity, Carroll adherence | Independent event identifier, date, duration and irrigation depth with a **different denominator** from recipients. |
| 5. Physical result | Established intervention outcome measurement | Water meter/soil-water/yield units, calibration and measurement window. |
| 6. Causal contrast | Established randomized/quasi-experimental outcome attribution | Require explicit comparator before savings are attributed to DSS. |

This is a proposed *claim provenance form applied to irrigation recommendation events*, not a new theory, validated method or measured improvement. The publication contribution cannot be certified absent empirical comparison to TIDieR-informed extraction (preferably prospectively preregistered); an interpretive Perspective might still discuss why this domain adaptation could be tested, subject to journal editorial selection and human author input.

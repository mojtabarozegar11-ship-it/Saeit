Source: Car NJ, Christen EW, Hornbuckle JW, Moore GA (2012), *Computers and Electronics in Agriculture* 84:132–143, DOI **10.1016/j.compag.2012.03.003**. An author-uploaded full-text transcription is accessible at https://www.researchgate.net/publication/257018309_Using_a_mobile_phone_Short_Messaging_Service_SMS_for_irrigation_scheduling_in_Australia_-_Farmers%27_participation_and_utility_evaluation . The prose preceding Table 6 says **40** irrigators answered the open-ended behaviour question, whereas the transcribed table reports five counts totaling **41**. This audit asks whether the displayed percentages are arithmetically coherent with either denominator, not which denominator is the true respondent count.

## Table 6 exact reconstruction

| Behaviour category | Displayed count | Displayed percentage | Count/41 | Count/40 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Small | 11 | 27% | 26.829% | 27.500% |
| Large | 10 | 24% | 24.390% | 25.000% |
| Significant | 9 | 22% | 21.951% | 22.500% |
| None | 8 | 20% | 19.512% | 20.000% |
| Marginal | 3 | 7% | 7.317% | 7.500% |
| **Total** | **41** | **100%** | **100%** | **102.5%** |

- With **41** as the denominator, all five published integer percentages agree with ordinary nearest-integer rounding. In particular, 10/41=24.390% and 9/41=21.951%, matching 24% and 22%.
- With **40** as denominator, the counts sum to **102.5%**, and 10/40=25% conflicts with the displayed 24%. Thus the *tabulated* percentage column is internally consistent with **41**, not with **40**.
- The prose's derived 46% affected substantially matches (10+9)/41=46.341%; adding Small gives (10+9+11)/41=73.171%, and adding Marginal gives (10+9+11+3)/41=80.488%. These are **nested author-reported behaviour categories**, not objective water saving or verified event-level adherence.
- **Inference boundary:** 41 is the arithmetically supported *tabular denominator*, not independently verified questionnaire sample size. The prose's 40 remains unresolved. No original respondent records or publisher erratum have been obtained.

## Table 5 remains a separate, unresolved problem

The transcribed six counts 26+7+3+6+15+17 = **74** while the table prints **72**. Its displayed percentages 36+9.7+4.2+8.3+21+24 = **103.2%**. Groups 1–3 sum to 36, which is 50% of 72 rather than the adjacent prose's 68%. The coincidence **36/53 ≈ 67.925%** is *not* evidence that 53 was the intended denominator; 53/72 refers to a different self-reported decision-consideration measure. Do not silently reassign a denominator, swap groups or repair counts without the publisher original.

The abstract's mutually exclusive full-season/half-season/never data-return counts **45+13+14=72** must not be merged with the six Table 5 behaviour categories or the Table 6 open-question respondents. Likewise, the reported **80% usefulness** is an author construct, not a metered irrigation effect.

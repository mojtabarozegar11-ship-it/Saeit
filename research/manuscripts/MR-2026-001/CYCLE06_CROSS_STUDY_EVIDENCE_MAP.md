## Synthesis discipline
Three orthogonal axes must be coded independently: (1) actionable DSS vs generic WST vs autonomous control, (2) implementation stage: design, demonstration, monitored use, independent sustained use, (3) outcome provenance: metered M, operational proxy M-proxy, directly observed adherence O, self-report S, predicted P, unknown U. Each endpoint gets its own comparator and duration.
**No pooled adoption percentage, savings rate or causal effect is computed from this illustrative map.** A review's defensible contribution would be to test how often these distinctions survive study-level extraction, but novelty remains unproven until systematic retrieval and overlap checks.

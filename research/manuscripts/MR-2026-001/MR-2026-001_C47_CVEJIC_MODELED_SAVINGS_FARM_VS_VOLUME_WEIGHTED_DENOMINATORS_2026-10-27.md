Calculation: annual percent = 100*(A6 SUM baseline - A6 SUM alternative)/A6 SUM baseline. The A6 differences match the A8 SUMs to 0.01 m³. Pool by summing modeled volumes before dividing, not by averaging yearly percentages. Annual farm-mean minus volume-weighted percentage differences are 3.7394, 4.4878 and 1.5829 percentage points. Distinct estimands, not a demonstrated source error.

**Farm rows:** A6 and A8 enumerate IDs 1–35 but IDs **22, 28, 30** have '/' in all three years; hence **32 nonmissing modeled farm rows**. This is NOT the verified denominator of the paper's 83% self-reported 2020 IDSS use: 35 participating farms and 46 farmers emailed advice are separate reported populations, and the survey response flow is unresolved.

**Scientific interpretation:** Approximate 25% savings are scenario-modeled alternative replenishment volumes, not independently metered causal water savings or event-by-event recommendation adherence. The pooled 23.6621% is a secondary arithmetic descriptor, not a field result, uncertainty estimate, new estimator or publisher correction.

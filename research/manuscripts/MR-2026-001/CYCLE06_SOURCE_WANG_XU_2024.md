# MR-2026-001 — Source audit 08: Wang & Xu (2024)
Status: targeted published-article calibration, not systematic inclusion. Checked 2026-10-24.
Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-76620-6
DOI: 10.1038/s41598-024-76620-6. Scientific Reports 14, 24969, published 23 October 2024.

## Directly supported facts
- Study of **443 farm households** in Minqin, China; questionnaire-based analysis of social interaction and reported adoption of water-saving irrigation technology (WSIT). Source: Abstract, Conclusions and policy implications.
- Authors describe positive associations between social interaction and reported WSIT adoption; depth and frequency of interaction matter, with reported heterogeneity by irrigation costs and experience.
- **Technology construct** is water-saving irrigation technology broadly, not a confirmed computerized decision-support advisory service.
- **Outcome construct** is questionnaire-based adoption analysis; this audit found no direct metered water-use comparator or field-verified DSS recommendation adherence. Do not equate reported adoption with independently observed DSS use.
- Observational regression associations and mediation tests do not independently establish causality; confounding and self-report biases require evaluation.
- The dataset is stated to be available on reasonable request to corresponding author; it was not obtained for this audit.

## Eligibility boundary
Provisional **contextual adoption mechanism**, not a primary DSS field-validation record unless the full text or data show a separately identifiable irrigation DSS subgroup. Tag S-reported behavior; M-water=not extracted; O-DSS-adherence=not established; P=regression estimates (not predicted physical savings).
This paper complements Monteiro et al. (intention) and Amini et al. (participatory DSS pilot), but the three constructs must not be pooled as one adoption rate.

## Next verification

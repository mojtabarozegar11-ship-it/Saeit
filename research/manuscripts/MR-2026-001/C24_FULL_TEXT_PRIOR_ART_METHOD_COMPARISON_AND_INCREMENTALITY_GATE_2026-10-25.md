## Important scientific changes mandated by this source comparison

1. **Do not claim "first", "novel theory", "validated method", or "systematic review".** These full texts establish close prior art and overlapping concepts.
2. State the actual distinction from Ara: their economic DSS selection **explicitly excludes daily irrigation scheduling**, while MR examines individual daily-advice events and inferential claims.
3. State the actual distinction from Ofosu-Ampong: their tool-scale **subscriber/adopter** denominator cannot be substituted for the number of **applicable irrigation recommendations** or physical meter events.
4. State the actual distinction from Bounajra: their technology/field extraction table does not **explicitly** implement a six-domain claim-specific causal-evidence chain; do not infer no other framework does.
5. Separate **causal identification** from a physical measurement. A meter may record irrigation but cannot by itself prove that an advisory caused a change; causal attribution requires a design and assumptions.
6. Distinguish **action temporally linked to advice** from **causal compliance**. An observed irrigation event occurring after a message does not establish that the message motivated it; report competing constraints, baseline practice and adaptations.
7. Fix the six-domain terminology: the sixth domain is **counterfactual comparison/attribution**. **Persistence** is a separate *cross-cutting follow-up time horizon*, not a second undocumented seventh link disguised in item six.
8. Include a concrete **non-interchangeability demonstration**: Car's **45/72** = 62.5% full-season data-return participation; it is not the percentage of recommendation-specific advice events executed. Ofosu-Ampong's 19-tool count and subscriber bands are yet another unit, not a per-farmer irrigation-event denominator.
9. Validation study should prespecify the **primary outcome** (rate of unsupported inferential statements per assessed claim), blinded expert adjudication, random allocation of assessors to worksheet versus ordinary extraction, clustering by assessor and paper, inter-rater agreement and time burden. These are **proposals only**, not completed experiments.
10. Acknowledge **selection bias** in the eight illustrative studies and **access bias** from abstract-only methods; no numerical prevalence or cross-study effect size can be estimated.

# MR-2026-001 C63 — Cvejić: 35 participating farms are not a verified telephone-survey denominator

**28 October 2026 | Internal source triangulation | MAJOR REVISION / NO SUBMISSION**

## Newly checked independent bibliographic evidence
The independent bibliographic abstract indexed for Cvejić et al. (2020), *Agronomy* 10(9), 1238, DOI 10.3390/agronomy10091238, explicitly describes an irrigation decision-support system developed and implemented **on 35 farms** in Slovenia's Vipava Valley during **2016–2020**. Source: https://infra.eurekamag.com/research/071/061/071061017.php (accessed 2026-10-28). The article's publisher URL is https://www.mdpi.com/2073-4395/10/9/1238 ; publisher direct access was HTTP 429 in this retrieval, so the original questionnaire table and respondent-level data were **not** independently inspected in this cycle.

## Distinct populations and outcomes
1. **35 implementation farms** is a study-scale statement in the independently indexed abstract. It is not proof that all 35 completed the 2020 telephone questionnaire.
2. **35 candidate respondents** is the *conditional* denominator previously reconstructed in C56 from the published integer-rounded percentages and "6% (two farmers)" statement, under a common-denominator, nearest-integer rounding assumption. The agreement with 35 implementation farms increases plausibility, but is **not independent confirmation of the survey sample size**.
3. **46 farmers** mentioned for 2019 advice recipients is a separate time- and exposure-specific count; it cannot be silently reconciled to 35 farms or to the 2020 survey without recruitment/attrition records.
4. The reported **25% water, 24% energy and 24% CO2 reductions** are *modeled conditional scenarios*, not experimentally observed realized savings or verified recommendation adherence.

## Falsification / missing evidence
Request or locate the original publisher PDF's methods and response table, the 2020 interview frame and completion counts, and the 2019 advisory recipient roster definitions. If the 2020 survey sampled a subset, used question-specific denominators, or rounded nonstandardly, the C56 35-person reconstruction cannot be promoted to a respondent fact. No original respondent counts, water-meter data, event timestamps, field efficacy or author correction was obtained.

**Editorial wording approved for internal draft:** "An independently indexed abstract describes implementation on 35 farms; the same number is mathematically compatible with the reported 2020 survey percentages, but the telephone-survey denominator remains unverified. The 2019 figure of 46 advice recipients is not interchangeable with either."

**Scientific decision:** evidence provenance improved; G/D/R/A and novelty gates remain open; no submission or authorship assertion.

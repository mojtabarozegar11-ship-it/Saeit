## A reproducible, falsifiable claim-to-evidence protocol

The unit of review is a **specific published claim**, not a paper-wide score. For every claim record: (1) source and exact table/section; (2) observational unit and denominator; (3) timestamp and provenance of generated/delivered advice; (4) independently verifiable action versus reported use; (5) physical water measurement, calibration and units versus modeled output; (6) comparator, uncertainty and confounding; (7) repeated voluntary use after researcher support ends.

Use M for directly measured physical field outcomes, O for independently verifiable recommendation-specific operator actions, S for self-report or participant attitudes, P for modeled or counterfactual outputs, and U for unresolved provenance. Multiple labels may apply to distinct claims from one source. **Absence of evidence is U, not farmer nonadherence.** Model calibration against observations does not make the modeled savings M.

Five decision rules: distinguish farm/plot/person/event denominators; keep research-managed plot implementation separate from independent farmer adoption; never convert scenario water savings into realized savings; retain adverse sites and economically rational deviations from advice; and avoid claiming causal conservation without a defensible comparison.

**Worked contrast A — Amini 2025 (DOI 10.1038/s41598-025-26567-z):** Rounded Table 4 irrigation depths imply F5 DSS/control 363.2/616.8 mm: -253.6 mm (41.1% lower relative to control), but F7 1057.6/764.9 mm: +292.7 mm (38.3% higher). These are within-source arithmetic comparisons, not causal population estimates. Reported water-productivity discrepancies across tables/conclusion require explicit annotation, not invented corrections.

**Worked contrast B — Cvejić 2020 (DOI 10.3390/agronomy10091238):** 83% reported use (S) and 25% scenario-based water reduction (P) do not establish 25% measured farmer savings (M) or independently logged adherence (O). The initial 35 farms, 46 later advice recipients and unknown 2020 response denominator cannot be combined into one verified person-level rate.

**Worked contrast C — Car 2012 and Lorite 2012:** 45/72 season-long SMS data return is engagement, not independently metered adherence. Lorite had already compared actual irrigation against advisory recommendations over five years. The novelty claim is limited to an explicit claim-level provenance worksheet, not first discovery of advice–action gaps.

**Falsification conditions:** The proposed worksheet has no demonstrated added value if equivalent claim-event traceability is already available in prior literature, if independent domain raters cannot reproduce its labels, or if its use does not alter inference relative to existing frameworks. No empirical inter-rater reliability or validation has yet been conducted.

## SANRA six-domain editorial gate (qualitative; no invented score)

1. **Importance:** Explicit practical consequences for irrigation claims, farmer adoption and water scarcity — described in Introduction.
2. **Aims:** Distinguish measured water, observed action, self-report and scenarios — stated in Abstract and Introduction.
3. **Literature search:** Purposive Europe PMC/OpenAlex and targeted DOI work; disclose search dates, keywords and why eight cases were chosen. This remains incomplete in manuscript methods.
4. **Referencing:** DOI-backed sources and primary-source qualifiers; full author metadata and restricted-source details still need final checking.
5. **Scientific reasoning:** Counterexamples, heterogeneous denominators, study-design limitations and prior-art challenge documented; genuine author-level independent scientific interpretation still required.
6. **Appropriate endpoint data:** Preserve field-level negative findings, scenario/measurement boundaries and uncertainties; source-table verification for Car and some restricted studies remains open.

# MR-2026-001 C42 — Amini 2025 original-publisher full-method and F7 evidence audit

**Date:** 2026-10-26. **Source:** Amini, Emami & Dehghanisanij, *Scientific Reports* 15, 42480 (published 27 Nov 2025), DOI 10.1038/s41598-025-26567-z, original open full HTML: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-26567-z . **Access scope:** full publisher HTML, Methods/Results/Conclusion; not independently downloaded participant-level records or validated event logs. **Verdict:** measured plot outcomes documented, independent event-by-event adherence not established; source F7 inconsistency persists.

## Design and observation methods verified from original publisher
- 2021–2022 crop season, **eight monitored farms/orchards in four villages**; within each farm a control section used conventional farmer management, while a treatment section was scheduled with the DSS. The article explicitly states that the farmer **attempted** to implement the schedule as closely as possible and **adjusted irrigation dates and times to site conditions** (Results: Irrigation management). This is not a randomized, independently audited event-adherence trial.
- Physical irrigation-water measurement is described: WSC flumes and flow hydrographs for surface irrigation, 20-litre catch containers with stopwatch for sprinkler-nozzle flow, and graduated containers at dripper outlets. The article also describes gravimetric soil-moisture samples at 0–30 and 30–60 cm, flow rate multiplied by irrigation duration for treatment/control depth, and crop yield measured separately by plot at season end (Methods: Collecting data on water flow; Irrigation scheduling).
- Water productivity is defined as harvested yield in kg/ha divided by irrigation-water volume in m³/ha (kg/m³). A measured **seasonal outcome** is not automatically a separately verified timestamped match to each recommendation. Provenance tags for the reported seasonal treatment contrast may include **M**; do **not** award **O** without an independently traceable recommendation/event join, its denominator, and verification of any farmer adjustments.
- The article compares modeled DSS seasonal depths with FAO CROPWAT 8.0 and reports an average model difference below ±7%. **Model agreement** is not farmer event adherence or independently established causal generalization.
- The treatment and control are **within the same monitored farm**; the source does not by itself establish randomized allocation, blinding, independent event audit, or a population-level causal effect.

## F7 source-level discrepancy: do not silently repair
- F7 treatment reported **+292.7 mm** seasonal irrigation depth compared with control.
- The publisher's tabulated WP entries (previously transcribed in MR V5) **4.6 vs 6.0 kg/m³** imply **−1.4 kg/m³** if treatment minus control.
- In *Water productivity and crop yield*, the F7 paragraph instead says **−1.3 kg/m³**; later in that subsection another F7 comparison says **−1.4 kg/m³**; the **Conclusion** says **−0.1 kg/m³**.
- These values are mutually inconsistent for one unqualified F7 treatment–control difference. The **−1.4** calculation is an arithmetic implication of the displayed table, **not** an author-issued correction of other prose. No unpublished raw water/yield data were retrieved; hence no definitive correction is warranted.
- The article states that underlying datasets are available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request; this is not an open raw dataset supplied here.

## Claim-by-claim evidence chain
| Claim | Source supports | Not supported |
|---|---|---|
| DSS generated an irrigation schedule | Methods and application descriptions | Complete timestamped recommendations shared with this audit |
| Farmer used/adapted it in treated plot | Authors' field description; dates/times could be adjusted | Independently verified count of advice events followed |
| Plot water depth/yield was measured | Instrument and plot-level methods | A source-verified universal F7 WP difference |
| DSS improved outcomes for all sites | Heterogeneous contrasts including higher water use at F4/F7 | Universal saving or persistence after researchers departed |
| Algorithm agrees with CROPWAT | Publisher's model comparison (<±7% on average) | Field adherence or external calibration of each irrigation event |

## Manuscript decision
Use the publisher's concrete instrumentation and treatment protocol to distinguish **M** from **O**, and retain the unresolved 1.3/1.4/0.1 F7 conflict. This adds original-source methodological scrutiny, **not** new empirical observations or novelty of G/D/R/A. **MAJOR REVISION / NO SUBMISSION**.

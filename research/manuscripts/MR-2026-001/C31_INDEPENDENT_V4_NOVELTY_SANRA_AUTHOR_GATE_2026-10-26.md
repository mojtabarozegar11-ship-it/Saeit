## Critical method / claim-level appraisal

**Strengths:** V4 distinguishes eight purposive primary studies from 11 contextual citations (19 total); explicit non-systematic scope; retains unresolved Car Table 5 category counts, Cvejić 83% denominator, Amini F7 discrepancy; rejects causal interpretation of simulations; introduces a meaningful **active comparator** (TIDieR/Carroll-informed extraction with versus without event-linked data fields) for *future* falsification.

**Remaining scientific deficiencies:**
1. No preregistered prospective extraction comparison or inter-rater validation has been performed. The proposed improvement in claim classification is **unproven**.
2. Three case-study source methods remain abstract/preview bounded; original typeset Car table has not been independently inspected. The manuscript cannot report precise independent adherence denominators.
3. A reproducible historical search strategy for the *original* eight examples does not exist; later discovery logs cannot be presented as a systematic historical search.
4. Directly competing irrigation-specific measurement/reporting frameworks may already operationalize event linkage; targeted further source review needed before even narrow novelty is claimed.
5. No real human author intellectual contribution or final approval has been verified. A proposed human author line is not a contribution statement; no journal submission can be ethically authorized on that basis.
6. No signed/confirmed identity, affiliation, COI, funding, AI disclosure, copyright/rights, journal/APC acceptance or cover-letter authorization. No editor manuscript ID.

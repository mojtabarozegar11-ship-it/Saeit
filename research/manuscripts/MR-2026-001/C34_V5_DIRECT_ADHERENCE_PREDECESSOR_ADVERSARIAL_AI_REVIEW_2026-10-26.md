## Source verification and newly uncovered direct competition

1. **Darouich H, Simionesei L, Oliveira AR, Neves R, Ramos TB (2024), Agronomy 14:66, DOI 10.3390/agronomy14010066.** Publisher full article: https://www.mdpi.com/2073-4395/14/1/66 ; independent University of Lisbon bibliographic record: https://researchportal.ulisboa.pt/en/publications/assessing-the-impact-of-irrigasys-decision-support-system-on-farm/ . This **already tests farmer adherence to weekly plot-level irrigation DSS recommendations**, in 20 selected maize plots with multicriteria indicators. The publisher methods say actual irrigation depths are obtained by **weekly surveys via local water-board technical staff**, not automatically by independently calibrated meters. The DSS sometimes temporarily assumes compliance when input is late. Hence *comparison of advice and actual farmer irrigation management* is **prior art**; neither autonomous metered event-level adherence nor its absence can be inferred from the abstract alone. **Internal source-date discrepancy:** publisher abstract says comparison years 2019 and 2020, section 2.3 says 2019 and 2022; do not assert either as a settled second year without publisher clarification.
2. **Tapsuwan S, Hunink J, Alcon F, Mertens-Palomares AN, Baille A (2015), Irrigation and Drainage 64:228–240, DOI 10.1002/ird.1887.** Official Wiley record https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ird.1887 verifies all five authors, 2015 issue, 2014 online-first date and a **pre/post trial evaluation** of farmer-facing advisory bulletins. Only publisher abstract was used; detailed source-level implementation metrics cannot be inferred. Iterative post-implementation user feedback is therefore not original to MR.
3. TIDieR, Hoffmann et al. 2014 (DOI 10.1136/bmj.g1687), and Carroll et al. 2007 (DOI 10.1186/1748-5908-2-40) already define general reporting fidelity/adherence/exposure. The *irrigation-specific* predecessor above makes an even narrower originality test mandatory.

## What V5 may and may not claim

**Defensible:** a **proposed** claim-level provenance checklist that forces denominators for *generated advice events*, *confirmed receipt* and *independently observed irrigation actions* to be labeled separately, alongside units, timing and counterfactuals. Eight purposive cases illustrate why this distinction matters; 13 contextual sources delimit it.

**Not defensible as new discovery:** that farmer adherence has never been studied, that recommendations have not previously been compared with actual practice, that a six-stage fidelity model is original, that 45/72 SMS engagement equals advice compliance, or that the proposed checklist has proven value. The direct IrrigaSys predecessor specifically defeats the first two.

**Possible incremental research test, not performed:** a preregistered, assessor-blinded extraction trial comparing (a) a strong TIDieR/Carroll-informed irrigation source-review template with (b) the *same* template plus event-level source-key and three denominators. The trial must measure unsupported inference, assessor agreement and cost/time, and allow null or unfavorable results. No effect size, reliability coefficient, participant count or causal benefit is invented.

## 26 October 2026 AI-only adversarial reassessment — Lorite economic adoption overlap (C43)

The independently checked [Lorite et al. 2012 published abstract](https://ideas.repec.org/a/spr/waterr/v26y2012i8p2397-2419.html) (DOI 10.1007/s11269-012-0023-3) does not merely compare actual and recommended seasonal irrigation via ARIS LIAS: it also reports **crop-specific irrigation water productivity (IWP) and irrigation water benefit (IWB)**. The prior-art [C43 evidence audit](C43_LORITE_2012_ECONOMIC_ADOPTION_PRIOR_ART_AND_EVENT_FIDELITY_LIMITS_2026-10-26.md) and the corrected MR V5 manuscript make this collision explicit.

**Critical finding:** a paper claiming originality from joining *advisory acceptance*, *actual irrigation* and *farm economics* would overlook a 2012 predecessor. The potentially distinguishable contribution is narrower: a prospectively defined, independently auditable **per-recommendation event matching protocol** and a measured incremental value relative to seasonal ARIS and economics. Neither a real matched event dataset nor empirical incremental validity has been supplied. A simple many-to-one mapping proof is explanatory logic, not a new estimator or a demonstrated effect.

**Required falsification before acceptance:** (a) original Lorite full Methods and raw observation units; (b) an actual G/D/R/A field data sample with prespecified matching tolerance and missingness denominator; (c) comparison against seasonal ARIS, IWP and IWB with independent assessment of event fidelity; (d) inter-rater reliability, farmer-level heterogeneity and publication originality. None is completed. **No new SANRA score is asserted without a full instrument-based reassessment**; prior 9/12 diagnostic is historical, not newly recalculated. **Decision remains MAJOR REVISION / NO SUBMISSION.** This is internal AI criticism, not human peer review.

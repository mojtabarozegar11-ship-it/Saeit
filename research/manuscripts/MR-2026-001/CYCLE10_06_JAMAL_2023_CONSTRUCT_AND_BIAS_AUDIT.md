# MR-2026-001 | Cycle 10 | Source-specific evidence quality and construct audit
Source https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/2023WR035810 (Jamal et al., 2023), §§3–4, Tables 1–2 and Appendix B.
| Claim/construct | Directly evidenced | Not demonstrated by this study |
|---|---|---|
| Field sensing | Field soil moisture/LAI measured at two maize sites; source §3 | Independent field-level DSS treatment effect |
| Algorithmic comparison | Optimized vs historical actual schedule, Table 1 | Measured water saving under an assigned optimized schedule |
| Behavioral engagement | Two 4-person groups chose virtual schedules; Table 2 | Real on-farm adherence to recommendations |
| Interest/acceptability | Debrief and farmer feedback, §4.2.2 | Multi-season voluntary continued use |
| Profit/crop production | Model-derived values for counterfactual schedules, Tables 1–2 | Verified treatment-attributable realized profit/yield |
| Participation | Ten attendees, eight workshop-group participants and eight survey rows as reported | Ten independent behavioral outcomes |
**Risk domains to chart prospectively:** virtual-to-field transfer, group rather than independent decisions, lack of randomized implementation comparator, model parameter uncertainty, single regional/crop setting, selection of invited farmers, uncertain post-workshop follow-up.
**Audit decision:** A publication's use of words such as “actual field” and “farmers' direct engagement” must not override outcome provenance; comparison baseline can be observed while intervention is simulated. Do not assign a global numerical quality score or declare the paper flawed without full context.
**Human inclusion decision:** PENDING. This is source-level charting, not formal PRISMA screening.

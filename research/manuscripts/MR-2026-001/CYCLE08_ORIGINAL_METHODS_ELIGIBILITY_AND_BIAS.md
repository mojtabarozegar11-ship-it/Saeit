# MR-2026-001 | Cycle 08 | Original prospective Methods draft
Prepared 2026-10-25; manuscript text for author review, NOT a representation of an executed systematic review.

## Eligibility and construct separation (draft text)
The review will distinguish an irrigation decision-support recommendation from the information systems that generate, monitor or implement irrigation decisions. The principal farmer-facing stratum will require evidence that a tool communicated an actionable irrigation recommendation (timing, depth, frequency or allocation) to a farmer or farm manager in a real production setting. Mere availability of sensors, irrigation accounting estimates, remotely generated predictions or fully autonomous controllers without an identifiable human decision maker will not establish exposure to farmer-facing advice. Such systems may be mapped in a separate technical-validation stratum if the protocol is amended and approved before screening.

We will chart separately: (1) recommendation generation and delivery, (2) recommendation comprehension and reported acceptability, (3) observed implementation or documented non-adherence, (4) voluntary use after study supervision ends, and (5) multi-season continued use. For each stage we will record the numerator, eligible denominator, data-collection window, observation instrument, and whether behavior was self-reported or externally recorded. Missing evidence of uptake will be coded 'not reported' rather than 'not adopted'. We will not pool intention, satisfaction and sustained use as one adoption outcome.

Field outcomes will be mapped to their provenance: direct metering or weighed harvest (M), observation of irrigation actions (O), respondent report or recalled volumes (S), model-predicted values or counterfactual simulations (P), and unverified (U). Mixed-provenance results will retain distinct labels. We will report study designs and comparators, farmer-selection mechanisms, baseline metering, missing data, intervention co-components, follow-up and season-specific weather conditions. We will not interpret a high model R-squared as water saving, or crop water requirement as applied water.

Illustrative calibration cases, not review results: Portoghese et al. (2025) use farmer survey and modeled water abstraction with a small metered validation subset; Tapsuwan et al. (2015) report pre/post farmer feedback on an advisory bulletin but no measured water-saving estimate in the publisher abstract. These cases motivate eligibility rules, subject to independent reviewer decisions after database retrieval.

## Methods safeguards
Eligibility, search platforms, final query strings and any deviations must be frozen and dated before screening. At least two suitable bibliographic databases, raw exports with hashes, deduplication log and documented human reviewer decisions are needed. Scoping reviews should describe study limitations and evidence gaps without automatically applying meta-analytic effect pooling. AI assistance is disclosed and is not counted as an independent human reviewer.
References: Portoghese et al. DOI 10.1007/s11269-025-04316-1; Tapsuwan et al. DOI 10.1002/ird.1887; Tricco et al. PRISMA-ScR DOI 10.7326/M18-0850.

## A. Narrowest defensible contribution, subject to author confirmation
A **critical narrative review of claim-level evidence provenance** in irrigation decision support, distinguishing (1) generation and timestamp of a recommendation, (2) verifiable delivery/receipt, (3) independently observed implementation, (4) physical field outcome, (5) credible counterfactual with uncertainty, and (6) voluntary reuse after active research support. This is a **proposed reporting checklist, not a novel validated metric**. Existing work on farmer adoption, actual-versus-recommended irrigation and smartphone advice prevents any broad first-in-field claim.

## Adversarial scientific consequence
A draft cannot imply that daily recommendation **generation, smartphone delivery, or field comparison with farmer practice** are unprecedented. The Elia 2022 publisher abstract already states all three. The proposed G/D/R/A provenance worksheet may still offer a reporting distinction between generated, sent, demonstrably received and independently executed actions, but its *incremental utility and reliability* remain unvalidated. The published seasonal 24% and 7% contrasts must be attributed to the original authors, not described as independent causal measurements made by the MR project.

# MR-2026-001 | Cycle 10 | Original prospective Methods text: separating virtual adherence from real-world adoption
**Status: manuscript METHODS DRAFT, NOT REVIEW RESULTS.** Human author and search specialist approval required before protocol freeze.
## Operational definition and charting
We will distinguish the physical location of data acquisition from the setting in which irrigation decisions are made. An investigation may use genuine farm observations to calibrate an algorithm while evaluating recommended schedules only in simulation or in a virtual farmer exercise. Such investigations will be coded as field-data-supported technical validation and, when applicable, simulated behavioral engagement; they will not automatically be classified as real-world DSS adoption. For each study, the evidence chart will separately record (a) where and how agronomic observations were collected, (b) whether an actionable recommendation reached a farmer responsible for an operating farm, (c) whether the farmer's subsequent irrigation action was independently observed, and (d) whether water, yield, energy or financial outcomes were measured after implementation. We will preserve the distinction between actual historical comparators and hypothetical DSS-guided alternatives even when both appear in one results table.
Where participants accept or reject a recommendation in a workshop, a virtual adherence measure may be calculated only with the source's explicit opportunity denominator and labelled as **virtual**. Group-level responses will not be treated as independent individual responses; invitation/attendance and analyzed-group denominators will be kept separate. We will not interpret favorable workshop perceptions as multi-season voluntary use. Farm-level adoption will require documented operational deployment and an identifiable decision maker, while sustained adoption will require a reported follow-up period beyond supervised introduction. No unreported adoption outcome will be coded as zero.
The design, setting, intervention implementation, comparator, observation unit, measurement provenance and uncertainty will be charted for every outcome. Claims of causal field water savings will require an appropriate implemented comparison; otherwise descriptive or modeled comparisons will be reported without causal attribution. This approach follows the principle of transparent source-specific reporting in PRISMA-ScR (Tricco et al. 2018, DOI 10.7326/M18-0850), and is calibrated against Jamal et al. 2023 (https://doi.org/10.1029/2023WR035810). This paragraph does not claim any review search, screening, included-study count or synthesis has been completed.

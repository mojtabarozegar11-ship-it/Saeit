## Material correction already incorporated in the manuscript

The earlier C76 v2 manuscript **overstated three Car (2012) numerical contradictions** by testing the 46%, 73% and 80% *influence* claims exclusively against the 72-person *Table 5* denominator. The subsequent C78 pack overcorrected by treating an alternative numerical match as proof of a Table 6 source crosswalk. Inspection of the [author-uploaded printed text, pp. 139–141](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/257018309_Using_a_mobile_phone_Short_Messaging_Service_SMS_for_irrigation_scheduling_in_Australia_-_Farmers%27_participation_and_utility_evaluation) shows that the **five unnumbered behavior categories of Table 6** can reproduce the percentages arithmetically, **without establishing that they are the categories referenced by the source's numbered-group prose**:

| Behavior class in printed Table 6 | Count | Evidence meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Small | 11 | Reported small influence / reassurance |
| Large | 10 | Reported large change |
| Significant | 9 | Reported influence with change magnitude unclear |
| None | 8 | Reported no change |
| Marginal | 3 | Reported educational influence without changed action |
| **Total printed Table 6** | **41** | **Printed preceding prose says 40 respondents — unresolved** |

**Reproducible candidate arithmetic, NOT a verified crosswalk:** Large + Significant = 19/41 = **46.34%**; plus Small = 30/41 = **73.17%**; plus Marginal = 33/41 = **80.49%**. However, the source's influence paragraph refers to numbered Groups **2 and 3**, then **1**, then **5**, whereas Table 6's five categories have **no numbers**. Its neighboring Table 5 does have numbered groups. Thus the earlier assertion that Table 6 definitively resolves the source attribution is **withdrawn**. The three rates are excluded from quantitative synthesis; only a possible arithmetic match can be noted pending original source confirmation.

**Still unresolved:** Table 5 counts 26+7+3+6+15+17 = **74** against printed 72; printed percentages sum **103.2%**; Group 4/5 prose-versus-table labels are reversed; first three Table 5 counts 36/72 = 50% versus prose 68%; Table 6 printed 41 versus preceding prose 40; Table 5 “never sent” 17 versus [UNE institutional abstract](https://rune.une.edu.au/entities/publication/68f9d1ef-6e62-415a-b831-0beb96df9853) 14. **No invented corrected counts, causal irrigation-savings inference or recommendation-event adherence rate.**

## Author-ready corrected paragraph (may be incorporated into final manuscript)

> The Car et al. (2012) author-uploaded report includes a 72-person water-balance grouping (Table 5) and an unnumbered 41-response behavior-change grouping (Table 6), although the accompanying prose states 40 respondents. The reported 46%, 73%, and 80% influence sequence can be reconstructed arithmetically from selected Table 6 categories (19/41, 30/41, 33/41). However, the source's influence paragraph refers to numbered Groups 2/3, 1 and 5, and no authenticated correspondence to Table 6's unnumbered categories has been established. The arithmetic match therefore cannot resolve attribution or justify a verified behavioral prevalence. Table 5's six counts total 74 against a printed 72, with conflicting group labels. Engagement, perceived usefulness, reported behavior change and independently observed irrigation implementation remain distinct constructs.

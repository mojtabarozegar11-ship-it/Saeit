## Material correction already incorporated in the manuscript

The previous C76 v2 manuscript **incorrectly asserted three additional Car (2012) numerical contradictions** because it used the 72-person *Table 5* denominator for the article's 46%, 73% and 80% *behavior-change* claims. Inspection of the [author-uploaded printed text, pp. 139–141](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/257018309_Using_a_mobile_phone_Short_Messaging_Service_SMS_for_irrigation_scheduling_in_Australia_-_Farmers%27_participation_and_utility_evaluation) reveals that the **five behavior categories of Table 6** reproduce the percentages, subject to an unconfirmed numbering-to-label crosswalk:

| Behavior class in printed Table 6 | Count | Evidence meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Small | 11 | Reported small influence / reassurance |
| Large | 10 | Reported large change |
| Significant | 9 | Reported influence with change magnitude unclear |
| None | 8 | Reported no change |
| Marginal | 3 | Reported educational influence without changed action |
| **Total printed Table 6** | **41** | **Printed preceding prose says 40 respondents — unresolved** |

**Reproducible crosswalk:** Large + Significant = 19/41 = **46.34%**; plus Small = 30/41 = **73.17%**; plus Marginal = 33/41 = **80.49%**. The manuscript now **withdraws** the false comparison with Table 5. The author must confirm whether the influence paragraph's group numbers refer to these Table 6 classes; the arithmetic alone does not prove the implicit mapping.

**Still unresolved:** Table 5 counts 26+7+3+6+15+17 = **74** against printed 72; printed percentages sum **103.2%**; Group 4/5 prose-versus-table labels are reversed; first three Table 5 counts 36/72 = 50% versus prose 68%; Table 6 printed 41 versus preceding prose 40; Table 5 “never sent” 17 versus [UNE institutional abstract](https://rune.une.edu.au/entities/publication/68f9d1ef-6e62-415a-b831-0beb96df9853) 14. **No invented corrected counts, causal irrigation-savings inference or recommendation-event adherence rate.**

## Author-ready corrected paragraph (may be incorporated into final manuscript)

> The Car et al. (2012) author-uploaded report separates a 72-participant irrigation-data classification (Table 5) from a 41-response behavior-change classification (Table 6). The reported 46%, 73% and 80% influence sequence is numerically consistent with the latter: 19/41, 30/41 and 33/41, respectively, although the prose refers to 40 respondents and the group-number crosswalk is not explicit. By contrast, Table 5's six counts total 74 despite a printed total of 72 and contain inconsistent group labels. These source discrepancies prevent robust estimation of behavioral prevalence or recommendation-event compliance. The reported usefulness, participation and behavior-change constructs should not be conflated with independently measured water savings.

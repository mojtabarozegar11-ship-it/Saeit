## Resolved to the limits of published evidence
1. **Amini F7 rounding order:** published F7 yield/depth gives WP 4.62368 vs 5.96156 kg/m³, difference −1.33789; rounding after division yields −1.3, while subtraction of Table 4's one-decimal WP cells yields −1.4. These two numbers are arithmetically compatible. Source: [Amini original PDF](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-26567-z.pdf), Tables 4–5. This does not resolve Table 5 −3.22 (percent-labelled) or Conclusion −0.1 kg/m³.
2. **Cvejić scenario:** the original [2020 publisher abstract](https://doi.org/10.3390/agronomy10091238) describes 25% irrigation-volume, 24% energy and 24% CO₂ reductions as conditional simulations, not measured realized outcomes. C135 revises the manuscript to make the 85% soil-water-reservoir condition explicit.
3. **Amini participant frames:** the four-village participatory selection, later workshops and eight measured farm cases are not assumed to be one unique respondent sample; no roster has been authenticated.

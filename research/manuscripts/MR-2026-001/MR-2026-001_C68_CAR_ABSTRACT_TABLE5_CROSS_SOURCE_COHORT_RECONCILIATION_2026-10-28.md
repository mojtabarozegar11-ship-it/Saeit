## Primary institutional record
University of New England's published institutional record of Car et al. (2012), https://researchers.une.edu.au/en/publications/using-a-mobile-phone-short-messaging-service-sms-for-irrigation-s-5/ , states **72 participants: 45 returned irrigation/rainfall data all season, 13 for half the season, 14 never returned any data**. ## Newly explicit cross-source discrepancy
The **institutional abstract's 14 never-returned-data participants** and **Table 5's 17 Never sent group members** are not numerically interchangeable. It is **unknown whether the authors intended different definitions, whether the author-uploaded PDF contains a typographical/table-layout problem, or whether a different analysis subset was used**. Do not claim a confirmed publisher erratum, and do not replace either value with an invented correction.

## Editorial decision for MR
- Report the internally coherent abstract's 45/13/14 **only as data-return engagement**, explicitly attributed to the institutional abstract.
- Treat Table 5 behavior-group percentages and totals as **unresolved**; do not compute adherence prevalence from them.
- Do not pool 53/72 self-reported consideration, 45/72 full-season reporting, 14 never-returned-data, or Table 5's 17 Never sent as though the constructs/denominators were identical.
- Obtain the **original publisher page image** of Table 5 and a source definition for group 6; if unresolved, preserve a transparent limitation rather than a corrected percentage.

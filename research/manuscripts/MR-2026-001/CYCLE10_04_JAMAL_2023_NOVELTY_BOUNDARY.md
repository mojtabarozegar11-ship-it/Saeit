# MR-2026-001 | Cycle 10 | New overlap / novelty boundary: Jamal et al. (2023)
**Verified source:** Jamal A, Cai X, Qiao X, Garcia L, Wang J, Amori A, Yang H. *Real-Time Irrigation Scheduling Based on Weather Forecasts, Field Observations, and Human-Machine Interactions*. Water Resources Research 59(12), e2023WR035810 (2023). DOI https://doi.org/10.1029/2023WR035810. Publisher https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/2023WR035810.
**Overlap:** The study already explicitly integrates a decision-support recommendation, farmer choice, and interactive feedback. Consequently MR-2026-001 **cannot** claim to be first to connect irrigation DSS with human decision-making.
**Crucial distinction:** Its workshop is described in §2.3 as a virtual irrigation exercise (VIE); §4.2.1/Table 2 report virtual group schedules and modelled yields/profits, not observed season-long DSS-guided irrigation on farms. Two actual fields provide model inputs/validation and recorded comparator schedules, not an implemented prospective DSS intervention.
**Prospective incremental question (not a proven novelty claim):** Among real farmer-facing deployments, how often are (i) recommendation delivered, (ii) implementation independently observed, (iii) water/yield/economic changes directly measured under a credible comparator, and (iv) use maintained beyond supervised testing? A full systematic overlap search remains required.
**Eligibility calibration only:** The Jamal record may contribute to a contextual virtual-exercise stratum and field-algorithm-validation stratum. Do not classify its virtual farmer responses as observed on-farm adoption or formally include/exclude before human protocol review.
**No original efficacy experiment, adoption rate or pooled saving is claimed for MR-2026-001.**

# MR-2026-001 C64 — Independent institutional SPON operational follow-up and evidence boundary

**28 October 2026 | Source-level critical audit | MAJOR REVISION / NO SUBMISSION**

## Fresh source and question
The University of Ljubljana Biotechnical Faculty's public PRO-PRIDELAVA project page (accessed 28 October 2026), https://www.bf.uni-lj.si/sl/raziskave/raziskovalni-projekti/140/povecanje-produktivnosti-kmetijske-pridelave-z-ucinkovito-in-trajnostno-rabo-vode-propridelava , describes a 2018–2021 applied irrigation support project and states that the SPON decision-support system became operational within Slovenia's environmental agency ARSO in **December 2021**. The institution reports transfer/integration, probe maintenance, farmer training and practical demonstrations in named crops. Separately, the SPON system's explanatory page https://www.spon.si/spon-sistem-za-podporo-namakanju/zakaj-spon (accessed 28 October 2026) describes recommendation generation from crop phenology, soil-water retention, measured soil water and multi-day weather forecasts, including replenishment to **85%** rather than 100% of field capacity.

These pages are **institutional implementation statements**, not independently audited experimental datasets. They are temporally and methodologically related to Cvejić et al. (2020), *Agronomy* 10(9), 1238, https://doi.org/10.3390/agronomy10091238 , but do not establish that the same farms, respondents or treatment units were followed prospectively from the 2020 study to 2021/2026.

## Claim-level findings
1. **New source-level prior-art constraint:** ongoing operational integration and advisory dissemination are documented as institutional project outputs. A manuscript cannot imply that the idea of moving an irrigation DSS into real public advisory service is unprecedented.
2. **Not established:** the institutional account does not expose timestamped farmer-specific G (generated recommendation), D (delivered), R (received), A (action) records linked to calibrated meters and independent outcomes. It therefore does not validate the proposed MR event chain.
3. **Not established:** operational status or system adoption does not prove water savings attributable to the tool, long-term individual farmer adherence, or a controlled comparison.
4. **Source distinction:** the 2020 paper reports 35 participating farms and 2020 telephone feedback, with a separate statement about 46 advice recipients in 2019. The later project site describes a different implementation/reporting period and does **not** resolve the respondent denominator.
5. **Intervention versus outcome:** 85%-of-field-capacity replenishment is a recommended management rule; modeled 25% water, 24% energy and 24% CO2 reductions in the 2020 paper must not be promoted to independently measured causal field effects.
6. **New editorial action:** cite this follow-up as a *contextual institutional source* (not an additional primary experimental case) and explicitly narrow claims about deployment and uptake. Future study must retrieve actual implementation logs and ascertain sample overlap.

## Evidence grades
- Institutionally reported SPON transfer/operation: **project-level operational statement**, corroboration required for exact deployment performance.
- Farmer-level receipt and action: **unverified**.
- Independently measured water/energy/CO2 causal effect: **unverified**.
- Cvejić 2020 telephone survey denominator: **still unverified**.

**Decision:** sharpen novelty boundary; maintain critical narrative review / NO SUBMISSION. No new experiment, new DOI, or claim of journal-ready novelty.

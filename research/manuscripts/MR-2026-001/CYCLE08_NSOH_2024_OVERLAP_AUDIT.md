# MR-2026-001 | Cycle 08 | New overlap audit: Nsoh et al. (2024)
Primary source: Nsoh B, Katimbo A, Guo H, et al. Internet of Things-Based Automated Solutions Utilizing Machine Learning for Smart and Real-Time Irrigation Management: A Review. Sensors 2024;24(23):7480. DOI 10.3390/s24237480. Full text https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11644754/
Verified source: Abstract; §1 objectives; §2 Review Methodology (Figure 1 and Table 1); text near lines 196–225 in web rendering.
Existing review question: integration of IoT/ML across automated irrigation pipeline, data acquisition, control, interoperability, cybersecurity and technical performance. Source reports discovery of 1,485 candidate items, 462 full-text candidates, initial selection 123, final 166 papers/standards/resources after supplementary discovery. These are AUTHORS' reported counts, not independent bibliographic verification.
Substantial overlap: digital irrigation DSS/automation, technical validation, claimed water efficiency, scalability. A generic 'AI irrigation DSS benefits' review would duplicate much of this scope.
Candidate distinction to test, NOT proven novelty: prospective MR-2026-001 centers observed farmer recommendation receipt, implementation/adherence, voluntary sustained use and outcome measurement provenance, not end-to-end automation architecture.
Boundary: Nsoh is a SECONDARY REVIEW for novelty/citation chasing, not a primary study eligible for farmer-level effect extraction. Fully autonomous controllers must be a separate stratum if included at all. Their reported savings are not to be imported into our synthesis without checking cited primary sources.
Novelty decision: CONDITIONAL / HOLD. More overlapping reviews, database searches and actual eligibility screening are mandatory. No novelty or originality guarantee.

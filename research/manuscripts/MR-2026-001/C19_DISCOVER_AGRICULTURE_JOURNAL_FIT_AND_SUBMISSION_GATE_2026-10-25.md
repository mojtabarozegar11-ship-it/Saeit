## Official journal policy checked
The [Discover Agriculture submission guidelines](https://link.springer.com/journal/44279/submission-guidelines) explicitly accept both **Review** and **Perspective** article types. The journal defines **Review** as a critical and **comprehensive survey** of a topic of major interest, with no length limit. It defines **Perspective** as scholarly review/discussion when the scope is **too narrow** for a comprehensive review or the work argues a forward-looking/controversial hypothesis. The present MR manuscript uses **eight deliberately selected cases**, not an exhaustive corpus; it is therefore **more defensibly a Perspective under this journal's categories** unless its evidence coverage is broadened substantially. This is a *fit recommendation*, not a unilateral change to the author-approved scientific approach. The underlying methodology must remain a purposive critical narrative synthesis; do not relabel it systematic/scoping without actually completing those methods.

Official guidelines require **an abstract of fewer than 250 words**, a **manuscript file**, and a **cover letter**. The C19 clean manuscript's abstract was independently checked at **248 words**. The journal requires a **funding statement** and a **data-availability statement**, with other relevant ethical and competing-interest declarations and required policies. Peer review is **single-anonymous**. None of these requirements implies automatic editorial acceptance.

The [publisher's current fee page](https://link.springer.com/journal/44279/how-to-publish-with-us), checked 25 October 2026, lists an **article processing charge on acceptance** of **£990 / US$1,390 / €1,090**, before applicable tax, and says waiver/discount applications must be made **at submission**, not later. The price can change and must be rechecked immediately before any approved submission. Do not commit the author to this fee or request paid services without explicit approval.

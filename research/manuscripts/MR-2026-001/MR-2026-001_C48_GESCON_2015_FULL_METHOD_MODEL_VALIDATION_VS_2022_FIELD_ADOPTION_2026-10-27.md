# MR-2026-001 C48 — original GesCoN method lineage: model calibration is not recommendation-event adherence

**27 October 2026. New original-publisher full-text prior-art audit.** The open 2015 Frontiers articles are distinct from Elia et al.'s 2022 commercial-farm Acta Horticulturae report. This is a source-reading and construct-validity audit, **not** a new farm experiment, metering record, full-text authentication of the 2022 trial or empirical validation of G/D/R/A.

## Primary publisher sources (direct full-text access)

1. **Elia A, Conversa G (2015), Part I**: *A decision support system (GesCoN) for managing fertigation in open field vegetable crops. Part I—methodological approach and description of the software.* Frontiers in Plant Science 6:319. DOI https://doi.org/10.3389/fpls.2015.00319 ; full original publisher text https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/plant-science/articles/10.3389/fpls.2015.00319/full (accessed 27 Oct 2026). The DSS integrates **daily water and nitrogen balances**, crop growth, ET and soil water/N processes; its recommendations concern timing and quantity of irrigation and N application.
2. **Conversa G, Bonasia A, Di Gioia F, Elia A (2015), Part II**: *A decision support system (GesCoN) for managing fertigation in vegetable crops. Part II—model calibration and validation under different environmental growing conditions on field grown tomato.* Frontiers in Plant Science 6:495. DOI https://doi.org/10.3389/fpls.2015.00495 ; original publisher full text https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/plant-science/articles/10.3389/fpls.2015.00495/full (accessed 27 Oct 2026). The abstract and Tables 1–5 report **five calibration datasets** from Foggia experimental trials and **13 validation datasets** from Foggia, Perugia and Florida. The validated outcomes were simulated-versus-observed tomato shoot dry weight, nitrogen uptake, total fruit yield and related crop development, **not** a per-farmer received/adhered advice-event fraction. Published validation metrics include SDW RRMSE **16.7%**, N uptake RRMSE **16.8%**, and NSE **0.96** for both outcomes in the paper's summarized validation. These are **authors' reported model-performance metrics**, not independently reproduced here.
3. **Elia A et al. (2022), commercial-farm application**: *Agronomical effects and response of growers in the application of the GesCoN DSS at the commercial farm scale.* Acta Horticulturae 1335, DOI https://doi.org/10.17660/ActaHortic.2022.1335.81 ; publisher abstract https://www.actahort.org/books/1335/1335_81.htm . Its abstract reports **six commercial farms** with DSS-managed versus grower-managed plots, mean seasonal irrigation reduction of 24% and N reduction of 7%. **The full 2022 methods remain unverified**; the 2015 papers do not supply missing 2022 plot allocation, calibration, meter provenance or post-support farmer-adoption data.

## Distinct estimands — why this changes the MR prior-art framing

| Evidence lineage | Unit/target | What the accessible primary source supports | What it does not support |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2015 Part I | Daily DSS recommendation algorithm | Existing integrated irrigation/N schedule logic, so daily recommendation generation is established prior art | Farmer received, chose or implemented each advice event |
| 2015 Part II | Experimental tomato model calibration/validation | Published model fit on 5 calibration and 13 validation datasets | Verified human adoption or event-level recommendation fidelity |
| 2022 six-farm application | DSS-managed vs empirical-grower plot contrasts | Publisher-abstract seasonal irrigation/N treatment differences | Exact event matching, durable autonomous adoption, independently verified causal effect across farms |
| Proposed MR claim-level worksheet | Each stated claim and its observed numerator/denominator | A **proposal** to separate model, treatment, communication and observed action claims | A validated new tool or proven superiority to existing reporting frameworks |

**Novelty consequence:** neither an irrigation DSS that issues daily water/N advice nor model calibration/validation is novel merely because this Perspective names those steps. The only tentative, still untested MR contribution is transparent **claim-specific provenance and event-level denominator disclosure**, subject to comparison against existing fidelity/adoption reporting frameworks and empirical inter-rater/field validation.

## Source-bound limitations and release gate

The original 2015 publisher texts were accessible, but this does **not** mean the full methods of the **2022** commercial-farm trial were inspected. Do not transfer 2015 model fit (NSE/RRMSE) to 2022 farm outcomes or confuse model validation with independent action validation. The original Lorite, Baroni and 2022 Elia full-method access gates remain separately open.

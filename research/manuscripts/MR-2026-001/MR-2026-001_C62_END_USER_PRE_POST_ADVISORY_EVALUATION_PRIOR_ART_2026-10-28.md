# MR-2026-001 C62 — Earlier end-user pre/post evaluation of an irrigation advisory bulletin

**28 October 2026 | New publisher-abstract-level prior-art screen | NO SUBMISSION**

## Verified bibliographic identity
Tapsuwan S. (2015 issue; first published online 29 November 2014). "Assessing the Design of A Model-Based Irrigation Advisory Bulletin: The Importance of End-User Participation." *Irrigation and Drainage*. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/ird.1887 . Publisher abstract and metadata: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ird.1887 .

## Source-supported methodological scope
- The study concerns a model-based irrigation advisory bulletin in the **Segura River Basin, Spain**, delivered to innovative farmers described as early adopters of modern irrigation technology.
- It reports a **formative evaluation** using **pre- and post-trial farmer surveys**, including acceptability/non-adoption factors such as relevance, flexibility, accessibility, confidence and institutional barriers.
- The abstract describes differences between farmers' perceived and actual information needs (including seasonal conditions, water quality, salinity and crop life cycle).
- **No objective event-level irrigation adherence, independently metered water savings, randomization, or exact sample size is established by the inspected abstract**. Do not infer any of these.

## Relevance to MR novelty
This is direct prior art for *end-user evaluation of irrigation advice before and after implementation*, more specific than generic technology-adoption surveys. MR cannot claim priority for collecting post-deployment farmer feedback or assessing whether advice meets user needs. The candidate G/D/R/A event-trace taxonomy remains a **proposed reporting framework**, not a demonstrated novel validated instrument. Whether this article documents an actual advice-delivery/receipt/irrigation-event chain cannot be resolved without the full methods and appendices.

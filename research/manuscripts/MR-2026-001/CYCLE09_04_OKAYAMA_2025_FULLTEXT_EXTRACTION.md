# MR-2026-001 | Cycle 09 | Okayama et al. (2025) full-text calibration
**Source:** Okayama A, Yamamoto A, Kimura M, Matsuno Y. Utilizing convolutional neural network (CNN) for orchard irrigation decision-making. *Environmental Monitoring and Assessment* 197, 168 (2025). DOI **10.1007/s10661-024-13602-1**, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11732880/ (PMCID PMC11732880).
**Scope:** real persimmon orchards in Tochihara I and Heibara, Japan. Leaf-camera images paired with measured soil moisture/pF values over two growing seasons. CNN classifies irrigation-needed vs moisture-appropriate; label based on pF thresholds, not a documented farmer compliance event.
**Provenance:** M = measured sensor pF and field images; P = model classification/prediction against labels; O = no directly verified farmer implementation of CNN advice in extracted sections; S = no farmer adoption denominator. **No measured water savings, crop-yield effect, or multi-season voluntary adoption can be inferred from classifier accuracy.**
**Methods anchors:** PMC article 'Model construction data', 'Soil moisture estimation model', 'Irrigation decision model'; Table 1, Table 3; holdout 80/20 approximate train/test split; cross-field validation described in Tables 10–11.
**Results anchors:** 'Results and discussion' / Tables 7–11. Same-district irrigation classification accuracies reported 0.93 (TIr) and 0.88 (HIr). Combined model accuracy 0.71; cross-field accuracies 0.50 and 0.51 (different model/site assignments). This is a **generalization limitation**, not evidence of realized farmer irrigation decisions.

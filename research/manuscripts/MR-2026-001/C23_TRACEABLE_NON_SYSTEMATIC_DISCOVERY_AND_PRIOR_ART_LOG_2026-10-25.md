## Specific scientific implication
A mere statement that DSS advice does not guarantee field adoption is **not new**: Baroni's 2019 publisher abstract expressly describes this gap; Jamal's 2023 design already models farmer decisions separate from recommendations; Car 2012 distinguishes message engagement from behavior. The candidate contribution, **if any**, is a *transparent claim-level provenance worksheet that refuses to infer a denominator or causal outcome from incompatible evidence classes*. This is an editorial framework proposal, not a validated new measurement instrument. It must be compared against earlier adoption reviews before any claim of unique contribution.

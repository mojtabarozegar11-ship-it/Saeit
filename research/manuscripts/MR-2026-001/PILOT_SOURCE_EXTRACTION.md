## Methodological caution flags to verify in the actual tables and supplement

1. **Small, site-dependent comparison:** eight monitored farms, split treatment/control sections, crop and irrigation technology differ. No general causal average should be asserted from the narrative.
2. **Measurement provenance:** irrigation depth and yield were described as field measured, while weather inputs and water-balance forecasts are modeled. Tag each outcome separately.
3. **Adoption vs exposure:** workshop participation or compliance during an intervention is not measured independent sustained adoption; code as pilot exposure and supervised use unless stronger evidence is verified.
4. **Numerical consistency:** narrative reports F6 water-productivity change as +0.15 and approximately +0.2 kg/m³ in different passages, F7 as −1.3 and −1.4 kg/m³, and the conclusion contains an additional basin-irrigation WP statement. PMC Tables 4–5 have since been checked (see addendum); the exact WP percentage/units remain unresolved.
5. **Uncertainty/statistics:** authors report a paired t-test and p < 0.05 in the results; the underlying units, multiplicity, site heterogeneity, and test assumptions have **not** been independently reproduced. Do not elevate this report to an independently validated effect.
6. **Participant denominator:** Methods mention workshop attendance while Results describe participatory target groups in a way that needs reconciliation with the eight monitored farms. Keep training participants distinct from monitored pilot sites.
7. **Cost outcomes:** no verified cost ledger, farmer income or independent long-term economic comparison has been extracted. Do not label perceived cost improvement as measured financial benefit.
8. **Full-text QA pending:** inspect publisher PDF imagery, data-availability statement and any supplement; reconcile full-precision water-productivity percentages in Tables 4–5, ideally with a second human extractor.

## Initial relevance decision

**Potentially eligible for a field-deployment stratum, pending protocol freeze and full-table verification.** This is **not** a final inclusion decision, a study-quality rating, a systematic-review result or an estimate of generalizable irrigation savings.

This pilot exposes a concrete question: how often do irrigation-DSS papers separately report (1) directly measured outcomes, (2) simulated output, (3) actual recommendation adherence, (4) participant perceptions, and (5) sustained independent use?

**Integrity boundary:** None of the numbers above may be presented as this project’s newly collected field data. Always credit Amini et al. and their source.

## Addendum — independently recomputed Table 4 irrigation-depth differences (2026-10-24)

**Second article repository checked:** PubMed Central full text https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12660781/ (PMCID `PMC12660781`; PMID `41309870`). Table 4 reports treatment and control gross irrigation depths. Calculations below are a reproducible **arithmetic check of published data**; they are not new field measurements. Formula: `difference_mm = treatment_mm − control_mm`; `change_pct = 100 × (treatment_mm / control_mm − 1)`. Percentages rounded to one decimal.

| F3 | 1064.7 | 1223.1 | −158.4 | −13.0 |
| F4 | 1460.2 | 1234.4 | +225.8 | +18.3 |
| F5 | 363.2 | 616.8 | −253.6 | −41.1 |

| F7 | 1057.6 | 764.9 | +292.7 | +38.3 |

**Result of this limited arithmetic QA:** All eight reconstructed depth differences agree with the broad directions in the article narrative, and with the Table 5 depth-change figures after treating F8's rendered `2.8-` as a negative value. Counts from these eight pilot fields must not be extrapolated to farms generally. The Table 5 heading says *reduction* but negative figures are used for reductions; interpret these as **signed changes** unless publisher clarification shows otherwise.

**Unresolved numeric/metadata checks:**

- Table 4 water productivity is displayed in kg/m³ to one decimal place; Table 5's final column is headed “Increase in water productivity (%)”. The relationship between that percent label and its entries is not uniformly recoverable from the rounded Table 4 values (for example F5: 8.4 vs 4.6 kg/m³ but Table 5 prints +3.87). It may be a labeling/unit/precision issue; **do not claim a proven error** before inspecting source calculations or obtaining clarification.
- Table 5's yield column values for F6 are both shown as 3.5 t/ha although narrative reports +80 kg/ha; this could reflect rounding, not necessarily a contradiction.
- F7's WP change is −1.3 or −1.4 kg/m³ in narrative, while Table 5 lists −3.22 under a percentage heading; a consistent denominator has not been established.
- Use observed *per-field* water depths and explicitly state source and method; avoid translating workshop-reported reduced costs into audited profits.

**Reproducibility:** All values above are transcribed from [PMC Table 4](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12660781/#Tab4), with arithmetic performed on the published one-decimal entries. [PMC Table 5](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12660781/#Tab5) is the point of comparison. This is a **one-record pilot**, no PRISMA source count or formal completed reviewer agreement.

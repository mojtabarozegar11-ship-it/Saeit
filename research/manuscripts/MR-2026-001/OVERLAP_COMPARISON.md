# MR-2026-001 — Prior-review overlap comparison, source-checked discovery

**Checked:** 2026-10-24  
**Evidence level:** Publisher-facing article metadata, abstracts and available indexed text, not a completed systematic retrieval or independent full-text appraisal of all reviews. **Novelty verdict: unresolved.**

The originally proposed broad title, *Field Validation and Farmer Adoption of Irrigation Decision-Support Tools under Water Scarcity: A Scoping Review*, risks repeating published reviews. The fact that an earlier review recommends more long-term field validation is not proof that a new scoping review is original.

## Testable candidate differentiation (NOT a novelty conclusion)

> Across primary real-world deployments of farmer-facing irrigation DSS, how are **exposure to recommendations**, **actual adherence or repeated use**, **observed water/yield/energy/economic outcomes**, **comparator quality**, and **predicted versus measured provenance** distinguished and reported, including neutral or harmful results?

**Decision table for whether a scoping review is justified:**
1. If earlier reviews have already extracted and compared these same study-level dimensions across field deployments, **do not** submit a duplicative review; refocus on a narrower original methods question or a genuinely new dataset.
2. If earlier reviews partly address them, identify precisely which dimensions and studies have not been synthesized, and test whether the incremental synthesis changes practical farmer/extension decisions.
3. If the remaining eligible studies are too few or too heterogeneous to support a responsible new synthesis, publish an honest protocol or methodological research note only if a journal explicitly accepts it; otherwise stop the publication claim.

## What remains before deciding

- Obtain and read full methods, search date/coverage, tables of included original studies, definitions of adoption, and data extraction of the closest **five** reviews above.
- Run a reproducible multi-database overlapping-review search, with verifiable records and exports.
- Build an overlap-by-primary-study table using DOI/record IDs. Quantify overlap only after actual matching; **do not invent percentages.**
- Have the named human author review whether the narrowed question is scientifically valuable and which domain/field perspective he can materially contribute.
- Freeze the final question and protocol only after the above. Do not change the scientific CV or claim submission.

# MR-2026-001 — Original methods-section draft (v0.1; NOT a completed review)
**Prepared:** 2026-10-24. **Status:** Proposed text for author/information-specialist review; do not represent planned procedures as completed. This section may only be converted to past tense after the documented procedures actually occur.

## Objective and design
This proposed scoping review will examine how empirical irrigation decision-support studies distinguish deployment of a tool from delivery and use of recommendations, and how they establish the provenance of water, agronomic and economic outcomes. The proposed unit of analysis is a **study–outcome–comparator**, allowing a single paper to contribute separately observed, modeled and self-reported endpoints without treating them as interchangeable. The design is informed by the PRISMA-ScR reporting framework (Tricco et al., 2018; DOI 10.7326/M18-0850), but compliance cannot be claimed until the review is performed and reported.

## Eligibility framework
The provisional population comprises crop farmers, growers, irrigation managers and advisers; the concept is an actionable irrigation recommendation or computerized/analytical control decision; the context is a real agricultural field or farm-facing deployment. Pure model experiments without field deployment, non-advisory sensors and water-behavior interventions without actionable irrigation decisions will be evaluated as contextual comparators rather than automatically pooled with DSS adoption studies. Whether **autonomous controllers** without farmer decision-making qualify in the main evidence map remains an explicit protocol decision. Outcomes will not be restricted to favorable results.

## Information retrieval (prospective)
Before execution, an information specialist and the named author should approve and freeze exact platform-specific strategies. The intended sources include at least two appropriate bibliographic databases (e.g., Scopus and Web of Science Core Collection or CAB Abstracts), plus reference chasing with provenance records. For each database, record exact query, platform, date/time, restrictions, exported raw citations, result count, and SHA-256 checksum. Web discovery and targeted source calibration do not supply PRISMA counts.

## Screening and charting (prospective)
After de-duplication with preserved record identifiers, title/abstract and full-text decisions will be logged with eligibility reason, reviewer identity and resolution. A second independent human reviewer should be used where available; AI-assisted classification must never be misrepresented as human dual screening. A piloted extraction form will capture tool function, delivery channel, farmer exposure, observed adherence, duration, water-constraint context, comparator, outcome definition and units, and whether measurements derive from direct instruments, controller logs, self-report or simulation. Mixed-provenance outcomes will be separated. The manuscript will state if screening is single-reviewer and describe implications.

## Analysis plan (prospective)
The planned synthesis is descriptive and stratified: (i) delivery/use/adherence, (ii) field-measured or controller-derived biophysical outcomes, (iii) predicted/counterfactual outcomes, and (iv) observed versus modeled economics. For each outcome, distinguish direct water metering from volume inferred from actuator runtime, and harvested yield from soil-moisture proxies. No pooled efficacy, adoption rate, effect size or cross-study denominator is planned without methodological justification and traceable extracted records. Null and adverse observations will be mapped, and earlier reviews compared at primary-study level to assess genuine added value.

## Constraints
No Scopus/WoS/CAB search, full-screening flow, independent human appraisal, original field experiment, review synthesis or novelty verdict has yet been completed. This is proposed methods text, **not a claim of research execution**.

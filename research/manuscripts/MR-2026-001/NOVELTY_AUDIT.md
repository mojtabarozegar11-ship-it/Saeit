# MR-2026-001 — Preliminary novelty audit

**Audit date:** 2026-10-24  
**Status:** Preliminary discovery only. This is not a systematic database search, protocol registration, evidence screening, data extraction, or a finding that the proposed topic is novel.

## Decision

**Hold the original title and novelty claim.** The proposed topic substantially overlaps existing reviews on adoption, agricultural water-productivity decision-support tools, and precision-irrigation DSS. A narrower question may be viable, but novelty is not established until a reproducible search of bibliographic databases and a full-text comparison of prior reviews are completed.

## Important primary evidence already identified

Amini, Emami & Dehghanisanij (2025), “Participatory evaluation of an irrigation decision support system for water-saving and productivity gains in Lake Urmia Basin,” *Scientific Reports* 15, 42480. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-26567-z). The study reports a participatory field evaluation across eight farms and four villages, using field measurements. It reports different water-use results by irrigation system and water-productivity changes. These are reported study findings, not results generated by this review. The paper must be fully assessed and its measurements, comparison design, and limitations charted if eligible.

## Provisional research question to test

> In empirical field deployments of farmer-facing irrigation decision-support tools under water constraints, what study designs measure tool performance, actual irrigation decisions/use, and observed farm outcomes, and how do reports distinguish field-measured outcomes from simulations or projected savings?

This wording is a **candidate for testing**, not an approved final question or a claim of novelty. It narrows the proposed synthesis toward the provenance and measurement of real-world outcomes, while retaining adoption/use only where the primary study actually measures it.

## Required next research step

Run a reproducible search in at least two accessible bibliographic databases appropriate to agriculture and engineering (for example, CAB Abstracts, Scopus, Web of Science Core Collection, or AGRICOLA). Record database/platform, coverage, exact query, limits, date run, export file, deduplication rules, and any citation-search steps. Search for and compare all relevant reviews, including those listed above, before finalizing eligibility criteria.

A general web-search discovery is not a substitute for database searches. Search-engine result counts must not be reported as PRISMA flow counts. No PRISMA-ScR counts, included-study totals, synthesis, or effect estimates can be reported until actual searching, deduplication, screening, and source-checked extraction are done.

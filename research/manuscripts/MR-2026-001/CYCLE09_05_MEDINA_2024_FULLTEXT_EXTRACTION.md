# MR-2026-001 | Cycle 09 | Medina et al. (2024) field-prototype boundary case
**Source:** Medina DE, Medina JD, Zorro JA, Medina Tobon D, Gomez JJ, Giraldo LF et al. A precision agriculture solution for water stress estimation in Hass avocado farms in Colombia. *Scientific Reports* 14, 31178 (2024). DOI **10.1038/s41598-024-82344-4**. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-82344-4 .
**Field setting:** working 'El Diamante' Hass avocado farm, Tolima, Colombia, described as 20 ha; prototype monitors weather variables and water-stress estimation in rural connectivity constraints. The paper reports weather collection 28 July 2023–30 July 2024 and three prototypes with approximately 12 months of measurements; a fourth installed 29 January 2024. See 'Materials and methods', Stage 1; 'Experimental results and discussion', Fig. 10 and Table 5.
**Evidence:** M = onsite weather measurements, rain-gauge failure/repair documented; O = real field prototype installation and development interactions, but **not observed longitudinal farmer compliance with irrigation recommendations**; P = potential yield and financial benefit estimates; S = informal interviews and user stories. User stories (6), (9), (14) concern weather, water stress, and water-use information.
**Economic boundary:** estimated subscription ~USD 15/ha/month and modeled potential revenue gains from improved irrigation (20–30% projected yield gain, USD 250–375/ha/month) are **projections/contextual assumptions**, NOT measured profit or treatment effects of the prototype.
**Open data:** Mendeley Data https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/k77y7n9yhg/1 ; dataset DOI **10.17632/k77y7n9yhg.1** (dataset is not an article DOI). Metadata says four devices and five weather variables. Dataset download and row-level audit were **not performed** this cycle.
**Provisional eligibility:** border case for farm-deployed decision-support precursor, not verified advisory adoption. Needs explicit confirmation that actionable advice was delivered and followed, and a human screening decision.
**Limitations:** one purposively chosen farm; descriptive field monitoring rather than controlled irrigation-outcome trial; prototype feedback not equivalent to sustained adoption; rain-gauge gaps and unequal deployment durations.

# MR-2026-001 | Cycle 07 | Original prospective methods: recommendation uptake and field endpoints
Prepared 2026-10-25. **This is proposed manuscript methods text, not a claim that screening, extraction or synthesis occurred.** Author and independent information specialist review required.
## Draft for Methods: implementation–outcome linkage
We propose to extract, at the study–farm–season level, whether an irrigation decision-support intervention generated a recommendation that could be acted upon, whether that recommendation reached the intended decision maker, and whether subsequent irrigation action was observed. Where authors only report access, satisfaction, or intention, the evidence will be charted as such rather than reclassified as adoption. We will distinguish supervised implementation within a research trial from voluntary use after researcher withdrawal and from sustained use across seasons. Each adoption claim will be associated with its denominator, observation window, ascertainment method and loss to follow-up.
Physical endpoints will be charted separately from behavioral endpoints. For applied irrigation, the extraction form will distinguish calibrated metering, calibrated proxy, operator log, farmer recall, and simulation; the same distinctions will apply to yield, energy and cost outcomes. We will not assume that a reported irrigation-depth difference was independently metered unless instrumentation is documented. Study comparisons will retain their original comparator (grower practice, concurrent control, before–after or modeled baseline), co-interventions and climate constraints. Repeated cuttings within farms will not be treated as independent adoption observations or as independent field trials.
Two contrasting seed examples motivate these extraction distinctions: Holt et al. (2025) report on-farm tool comparisons and variable year-specific outcomes, while Baroni et al. (2019) report positive user feedback but infrequent implementation of recommendations. These examples are not yet members of a systematic corpus and will not be presented as review findings until retrieved, screened and extracted under a frozen protocol.
References: Holt et al., DOI 10.1002/saj2.70101; Baroni et al., DOI 10.1002/ird.2337.
## Human decision
Working main population: farmer-facing advisory DSS. Fully autonomous control should be charted as contextual technical validation, not human adoption, pending explicit author decision. No final review question or eligibility freeze has been approved.

# MR-2026-001 — Cycle 11 — original prospective METHODS draft
Status: manuscript-methods draft, not results, not author-approved protocol.
## Outcome provenance and unit-of-analysis safeguards
We will code each evaluation at the finest level for which an independently observed comparator is actually available. A system may generate field-resolution maps or farm-specific recommendations while being validated solely against regional annual abstraction totals. In that situation, its spatial resolution will be recorded separately from its validation resolution, and aggregate prediction agreement will not be interpreted as proof of field-level calibration, realized water savings, or behavioral uptake. For every reported performance statistic, we will chart the number and temporal spacing of independent observations, the physical meaning of the reference measurement (e.g., water withdrawn at a well versus water applied to a field), and any spatial mismatch between prediction and comparator. A short series of annual observations will not be reinterpreted as a large field-level sample simply because the underlying model operates on many pixels.
For intervention evaluations we will distinguish advisory delivery, recommendation receipt, farmer decision, actual irrigation application and post-intervention measurement. A mobile interface alone will not be coded as adoption. Where a farmer-facing tool is tested in a commercial field but the published record lacks usage logs, adoption will be coded **not reported**, not assumed positive or negative. We will also retain agronomic null outcomes and reported uncertainty, including comparisons of yield under different irrigation regimes.
Two calibration cases motivating these prespecified distinctions are Bonet et al. (2026), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.agwat.2026.110131 (field trial; abstract-level evidence presently available), and Bernhardt et al. (2026), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.agwat.2026.110730 (aggregate model validation). These examples do not establish formal inclusion. The review's exact PCC eligibility and primary/secondary strata require author agreement **before** final database searching and human screening. PRISMA-ScR reporting will be used without fabricating flow counts.

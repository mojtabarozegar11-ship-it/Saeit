# MR-2026-001 — Journal fit, author requirements and submission gates

**Source check date:** 2026-10-24. **Status:** presubmission planning only; no completed manuscript, publisher submission, manuscript ID, or payment authorization. **Do not submit** on the strength of this checklist.

## Candidate 1: Discover Agriculture (Springer Nature)

- **Verified official instructions:** https://link.springer.com/journal/44279/submission-guidelines
- **Verified official publication costs:** https://link.springer.com/journal/44279/how-to-publish-with-us
- **Scope check:** Agricultural production, water resource use and precision agriculture are compatible with the journal's stated remit; editorial suitability of a narrowly differentiated scoping review remains untested.
- **Article type:** The instructions explicitly accept **Review** articles, without a fixed review-article length. Do not select “Research” or “Registered Report” unless the completed work actually meets that definition.
- **Submission platform:** **Snapp**; a manuscript, an abstract **under 250 words**, and a **cover letter** are required. The manuscript can be a Word file (figures/tables embedded) or LaTeX source ZIP.
- **Ethics / authorship:** all coauthors must approve publication and the corresponding author must declare contributions, competing interests, prior publication/overlap and permissions. The manuscript requires a **funding statement** and **data-availability statement**, and an ethics statement where applicable. If the review uses only public literature, document that fact accurately; do not assume a blanket exemption for future surveys or original human data.
- **Cost:** Publisher lists **GBP 990 / USD 1,390 / EUR 1,090** APC at the time of this check, plus applicable tax; charge is set at **acceptance**, not submission. Waiver/discount requests, if needed, must be made **at submission**. The author must decide on potential cost and any waiver application **before** final submission.
- **Format notes:** numbered references in square brackets, a consistent bibliography, minimum 12-point font, no more than three heading levels. Confirm latest requirements immediately before upload.
- **Assessment:** **Potentially suitable**, but a review that simply repeats prior surveys is not defensible; the contribution must be established with reproducible searching and actual study-level charting.

## Candidate 2: Agricultural Water Management (Elsevier)

- **Publisher journal and author-instructions entry point:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/agricultural-water-management/publish/guide-for-authors
- **Independent directory entry checked:** https://doaj.org/toc/1873-2283
- **Scope check:** Direct topical fit for agricultural water-management decision tools, field evidence and adoption; however, the journal already published several highly overlapping reviews (Ara 2021; Mabhaudhi 2023; Umutoni & Samadi 2024; Moustafa 2026). A compelling, non-duplicative study-level contribution is a **hard requirement** for a credible submission.
- **Review articles:** Prior reviews are published in this journal; the currently accepted manuscript categories, submission formatting and any presubmission enquiry requirements must be confirmed in the **live publisher guide** before final targeting.
- **APC:** The DOAJ directory entry visible on 2026-10-24 says **up to USD 3,510**, with a waiver policy. This is **third-party directory data**, not a verified current publisher quote. The official publisher guide could not be read in this check (HTTP 403); re-verify price and waiver conditions directly with Elsevier before any cost commitment.
- **Assessment:** Strong subject alignment but **high overlap/rejection risk** unless the novel question and complete evidence map genuinely add a new finding or method.

## Required author decisions when the evidence work is complete

1. Confirm the substantive scientific contribution and the final scope/novelty decision.
2. Verify the exact byline, affiliations, corresponding-author email, ORCID if any, coauthors and their consent.
3. Approve the final text, integrity declarations, target journal and fee/waiver terms.
4. Authorize a **single** publisher submission (never simultaneous submissions). Record publisher-generated submission receipt and Manuscript ID, not merely a drafted cover letter.

**No unsupported statement of authorship, peer review, database search, acceptance or publication may be added to the public CV.**

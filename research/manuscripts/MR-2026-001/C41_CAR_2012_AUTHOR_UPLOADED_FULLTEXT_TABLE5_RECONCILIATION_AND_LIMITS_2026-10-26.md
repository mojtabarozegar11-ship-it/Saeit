## Independent internal consistency issues

1. The neighboring narrative defines **Group 4 as 'Stopped sending'** and **Group 5 as 'Less throughout'**, whereas the extracted Table 5 labels **Group 4 'Much less'** and **Group 5 'Stopped'**. A prose/table label inversion is possible, but the source cannot be silently corrected.
2. The neighboring text describes **Groups 1–3 as 68%** of participants; the extracted counts sum to 26+7+3 = **36/72 = 50%**, not 68%. Neither the displayed group-count total nor the stated 68% is a defensible denominator for event-level adherence without original participant records.
3. The independent abstract-level duration groups **45 all season + 13 half season + 14 never = 72** reconcile correctly, but are **not identical definitions** to the six Table 5 water-balance behavior groups. They cannot be substituted as an arithmetical correction of Table 5.
4. A later **Table 6** is about *behavior-change interview categories* with **41 respondents**; its percentages and denominators must not be conflated with the 72-participant Table 5 or abstract groups.

## Decision for MR V5

- Retain the independently corroborated abstract-level 45/13/14 **engagement-duration** groups as such, not compliance or physically metered irrigation.
- Strengthen the existing Table 5 exclusion: explicitly report the extracted **74 versus 72** and **103.2% versus 100%**, the **Group 4/5 label inconsistency** and the **68% versus 50% discrepancy** with the above full-text-version limitation.
- Do **not** choose an allegedly correct group value, infer actual recommendation adherence, compute water savings, or allege a confirmed publisher erratum.
- Obtain the **original typeset Table 5 page image**, publisher correction or author clarification before accepting any six-category quantitative distribution as authoritative.
- This source audit improves integrity of a narrative Perspective; it does **not** validate the G/D/R/A worksheet, establish journal novelty or authorize submission. MR remains **MAJOR REVISION**.

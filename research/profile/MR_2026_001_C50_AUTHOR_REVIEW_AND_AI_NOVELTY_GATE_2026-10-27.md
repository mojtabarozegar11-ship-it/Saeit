## Adversarial conclusion: direct novelty collision
Darouich et al., *Agronomy* 2024 14(1):66, https://doi.org/10.3390/agronomy14010066, explicitly evaluate farmer adherence to IrrigaSys using farmer-vs-model schedule comparisons and multicriteria water/economic criteria. This defeats any claim that MR first recognized farmer adherence or first compared actual farmer scheduling against model recommendations. Their data are not proof of independently metered advice-event compliance: farmer irrigation input comes through technician surveys, and ETa/yields are modeled. This narrows, but does not automatically establish, an incremental MR contribution.

**New source discrepancy:** original PDF abstract says 2019/2020; Methods, Figures 4–5 and Table 2 say 2019/2022. Treat 2022 as the year in the body, but seek source clarification before relying on year-specific cross-study estimates. No correction to the published paper is alleged.

**New independently recalculated arithmetic from rounded published means:** 2019 farmer 7,800 vs model 8,650 m³/ha = −9.8266% relative to model; 2022 farmer 7,940 vs model 7,800 m³/ha = +1.7949%. Neither is a causal effect, a percentage of advice events obeyed or a measured crop yield contrast.

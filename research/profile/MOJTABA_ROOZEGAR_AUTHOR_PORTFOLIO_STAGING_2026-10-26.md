### Reproducibility and research integrity

The work emphasizes traceable public-source data, preserved computational scripts, independent internal quality checks and explicit separation of observed findings from proposals. The WDI project uses official historical data vintages; the wheat project uses original ENA FASTQ rather than converting FPKM/TPM to raw counts. AI-generated analyses are **not** independent human peer review. Data availability, journal policies, authorship and publication disclosures must be verified per manuscript.

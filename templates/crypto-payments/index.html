How to pay with TRX for a digital download

MojPlayWin digital products use a guest checkout that starts with an email address. You do not need to connect a wallet to browse products or request a checkout invoice. This page is guidance, not an invoice or a payment request.

1. Choose your product

Read the product details and price. Select only the item you want before opening its own checkout page.

Browse verified store

2. Enter your purchase email

At checkout, enter a valid email address and follow the server's instructions to request an order-specific native TRX invoice. Keep your order reference.

Buy this storybook with TRX

3. Check the invoice before sending

Use only the network, receiving address and exact native TRX amount shown in your own generated invoice. Do not reuse an address or send based on a screenshot, chat message or third-party instruction.

4. Await independent confirmation

Payment status and download access depend on server-side blockchain verification. An unconfirmed transfer does not grant a download. Keep the transaction hash and do not send a second payment while an existing order is unresolved.

5. Access or recover your purchase

Use the link or receipt provided for a completed order. If you change devices, the email-based recovery route is available; it may ask for a verification code.

Recover by email

6. Get purchase support

If payment remains unresolved, contact support with your invoice reference and transaction hash. Never share your wallet seed phrase, private key or email verification code.

Purchase support

MOJ PROP Starter30 registration and its proposed 30 TRX fee are not payable through this page. Starter30 has a separate official 30 TRX eligibility and invoice checkout. An invoice is conditional on identity and server-side payment checks; do not transfer TRX until that checkout issues your own unique invoice.

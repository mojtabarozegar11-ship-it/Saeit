SAMAN / ABOUT

A broader home for curiosity.

SAMAN is a MojPlayWin destination built around thoughtful play and practical learning. Chess is part of the story, alongside a wider ambition to bring together games, digital resources, courses, community and partnerships.

OUR PURPOSE

Make useful experiences easier to find

We organize related offers into clear paths: discover, learn, play, purchase or collaborate. Every product and program should explain what it offers and what a visitor can expect.

OUR APPROACH

Grow with care

New revenue streams should follow real value. We label future plans clearly, link visitors to the right MojPlayWin destination and publish commercial terms before enrollment or purchase.

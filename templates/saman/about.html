Transparency

Frequently asked questions

Is SAMAN only about chess?

No. Chess is a starting point alongside reasoning, creative skills, digital learning and collaborative projects.

Are all advertised activities active?

No. Every item is marked according to its readiness. Future membership, courses and public USDT checkout remain under preparation.

Can I earn guaranteed income by playing?

No. SAMAN does not promise income from playing games, and its free browser challenges involve no wagering.

Who runs SAMAN?

SAMAN is a destination within MojPlayWin. Links to the parent platform are provided for related experiences and updates.

SAMAN is continually developed. Service availability, product pricing and public enrollment are confirmed only on the relevant live pages.

A structured 60-day simulated challenge plan

Use the evaluation window to measure repeatable decisions rather than chasing a profit target or assuming that a virtual balance represents a funded live account.

What the MOJ PROP Starter 30 description does and does not mean

The publicly described evaluation lasts 60 days and starts with a $1,000 virtual reference balance. Both the challenge and post-pass account stage are simulated. The listed access price is 30 native TRX, subject to a valid server-issued invoice and complete accepted terms. The advertised risk figures include a 6% daily drawdown ceiling, 3% maximum per-trade risk, 4% aggregate open risk and a 50% best-day share threshold. These are separate constraints.

Days 1–7: establish the baseline

Read all currently accepted terms. Choose a consistent journal format, write down simulator assumptions, and define why a setup qualifies. Specify maximum planned loss before every entry, including fees and the combined open exposure. Record no-trade days as well as activity days.

Days 8–21: test repeatability

Separate planned actions from observed outcomes. Review stop placement, session selection, correlation and deviations from the written thesis. Use a smaller internal risk budget if needed; a maximum allowance is not a target to consume.

Days 22–42: review concentration and drawdown

Check whether one unusually profitable simulated session dominates net results. Inspect winning and losing sessions using the same criteria; do not delete negative days or double count gains. A positive cumulative result can still fail the published consistency rule or another limit.

Days 43–55: audit your evidence

Reconcile timestamps, instruments, unit values, planned loss, actual simulated execution, aggregate open risk, and the timing of each session. Document any disputed fills without assuming that an unrealized balance is a cash entitlement.

Days 56–60: close the journal honestly

Compare the full simulation record with every final criterion in the accepted rulebook. Eligibility and qualifying profit require the operator's documented verification. A displayed green number does not independently prove a successful pass or completed real reward settlement.

The five questions to ask before applying

  1. Exactly when does the 60-day clock start and end?
  2. How are daily and overall loss limits, resets and invalidation measured?
  3. What are the phase targets, minimum trading days and trade-count rules?
  4. How are eligible profit, the best day and prohibited strategies evaluated?
  5. What independent evidence supports the reward method, timing, recipient verification and dispute process?

Never pay an address from a chat message or screenshot. Only consider a transfer after verifying the official invoice details, the service scope and the complete conditions. Passing or earning profit is not guaranteed.

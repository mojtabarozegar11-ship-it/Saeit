Professional Trader Pathway · Separate from Simulated Challenge

Selected professional traders: live accounts, monthly salary and profit share.

Alongside the customer-facing simulated MOJ PROP challenge, the company has a separate pathway for professional traders it identifies, evaluates and formally appoints. Appointed professionals trade on company-controlled real-money brokerage accounts under company risk rules—not on customer-funded accounts.

1. Company selection and appointment

MOJ PROP identifies and evaluates suitable traders. Any live trading assignment requires an individual written appointment, identity and compliance checks, broker permission where applicable, and agreement on strategy, loss limits and authority. A trading challenge purchase or pass is not automatic acceptance into this pathway.

2. Authorized real-account trading

Following formal approval, selected traders execute permitted trades on a company-controlled real account using the company's own allocated funds. The company defines account access, position limits, risk controls, reporting, monitoring and the ability to suspend trading. Candidates are not asked to deposit investment capital with MOJ PROP.

3. Three components of professional trader compensation

  • Profit share: each appointed trader receives their agreed share of actual, verified net trading profits, calculated under their individual written terms.
  • Monthly salary: an agreed monthly salary is payable under the approved employment or engagement agreement.
  • Company-paid expenses: reasonable, previously approved work-related expenses are paid by the company or reimbursed in accordance with the agreed policy.

Salary amount, profit-sharing percentage, eligible expenses, payment dates, currencies, taxation and performance standards are specified individually. No amount or job opening is advertised here without a confirmed offer.

Important distinction: The USD 1,000 balance, challenge trades and post-pass simulation account advertised in Starter 30 remain entirely virtual. Eligible simulated-trading performance rewards, if payable under the published challenge rules, do not convert a customer into an employee or provide a real account. The professional trader pathway is a separate company appointment; no customer deposits are solicited.

Enquiries and selection information

The selected trader programme is subject to individual approval and operational availability; this page is not a public job vacancy or an unconditional offer of employment. For questions, use MOJ PROP's existing contact channel and specify Selected Professional Trader Pathway. Do not submit passwords, keys, private financial credentials or funds.

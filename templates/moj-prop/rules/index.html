RULEBOOK V1 / EXECUTION GATES

Rules before scale.

This page records the current commercial and risk gates. A page is not treated as ready until it has an owner, evidence and a next action.

Review-ready

Product definition

Product type, output, language, delivery time and responsibility boundaries must appear in the written offer.

Design required

Risk and drawdown

Daily loss, total drawdown, invalidation, resets and prohibited behaviour must be numeric and reviewable.

Legal lock

Live account and challenge

Until entity, country, contract, KYC, consumer terms and risk infrastructure are confirmed, no capacity or fee may be collected.

Payment lock

TRX and invoice

No public wallet address is displayed. Amount, lawful route, order ID, transaction evidence and delivery belong in the approved invoice process.

Fixed principle

No profit promise

Market content is educational. Commercial copy must not promise a guaranteed return, income or trading result.

Operating centre

mojtaba.bot.ai

Requests pass through one identity: intake, review, written offer, approval, delivery and receipt.

NEXT DECISION

What unlocks the next gate?

  1. Confirm the operating country and lawful entity.
  2. Write contracts, refund terms, KYC and complaints handling.
  3. Complete order, invoice, receipt and delivery records.
  4. Run an independent risk review before public financial activation.

## First-line support

The order portal provides actual answers for extraction, invalid units/margins, lost browser data and payment recheck. Unresolved questions create persisted tickets; owner answers are shown in the same portal. No AI answer is represented as a completed operational action.

## Day-one KPIs

- Qualified visits (human-qualified separately from approximate session count), attribution source/campaign.
- Checkout starts / estimated visits; orders / checkout starts; verified payments / orders.
- Gross verified IRR, recorded refunds, actual provider fee and taxes after reconciliation.
- Download attempts and at least-one-download per paid order; a successful HTTP response is not proof of customer use.
- First-value completion from voluntary feedback or observed user test; offline product has no hidden telemetry.
- Support tickets per paid order, first-response and resolution time; refund reasons.
- Customer-acquisition cost only with recorded acquisition spend; profit and ROI remain unknown until costs are reconciled.
- Repeat-use feedback, referral interest and price objections; v1 does not create recurring revenue merely because an account exists.

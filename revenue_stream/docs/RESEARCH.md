## Candidate comparison

Ratings are analyst judgments about current execution fit, not empirical probabilities. No other stream is implemented.

| Criterion | Existing academic AI report | Broad website/SEO service | Food-order CostKit (selected) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Observed demand evidence | No sales in sampled records | General market, no identified buyer | Multiple established/free costing tools; no Zomorod buyer yet |
| Speed to usable delivery | Incomplete 8/40 product, expert review needed | Requires qualified manual fulfilment | Deterministic small tool can be delivered immediately after verified purchase |
| Build/maintenance cost | Model/API and review costs | Recurring manual labour and customer access | Low server use, no per-use AI/API cost |
| Potential contribution margin | Uncertain production/review costs | Labour-dependent | High before acquisition, support, tax and development costs; not guaranteed |

| Global expansion | Translation plus specialist trust | Language and delivery staffing | Bilingual product; international payment still pending |
| Automation | Quality review bottleneck | High-touch fulfilment | Order/verify/download/report automated; support escalation human |
| Competition | High general AI content supply | Very high | High, including free tools |
| Plausible differentiation | Not yet demonstrated | Not yet demonstrated | Persian RTL, toman/rial, full-order costs and offline ownership; hypothesis, not moat |
| Legal/operational risk | Research claims, accuracy and rights | Access permissions, service promises | Bounded arithmetic aid; still consumer/privacy/payment obligations |

Selection prioritises quickest low-cost **testable** paid value with an actual deliverable. It does not establish that selling this tool will be easier than every alternative.

## Price and validation

Proposed initial price: 149,000 toman (1,490,000 IRR), one-time for v1 and one-business internal use. This is an owner-reviewable experiment, not a market-clearing price estimate. No fake discount, scarcity or guaranteed savings.
At one sale, cash before provider fees/refunds/taxes is 149,000 toman. Marginal model cost is zero. Support labour, acquisition, development and hosting are real costs and must be measured before calling any balance profit.
Do not introduce subscriptions until customers demonstrate recurring needs such as collaborative recipe updates. That is future scope, not another stream in this mission.

First validation: recruit 5–10 willing target users through owner-authorized contacts. Have each cost an existing 10–50 portion order using their own data, compare their prior method, record task completion, confusion, value and price objections. Ask for a real purchase after launch approval. Do not buy scraped lists or send unsolicited batches.
Decision thresholds are hypotheses: if 10 qualified discussions yield no meaningful need or willingness to pay, revisit positioning before ads. After 50 qualified visits, inspect checkout and objections; zero sales is a reason to investigate, not to fabricate conversion or expand features.

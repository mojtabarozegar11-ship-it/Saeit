TRX purchase recovery

Prepare a safe support request when your invoice, purchase history, or download is unavailable. Works offline; no account, wallet connection, or blockchain access required.

Already sent TRX? Do not send a second payment while the first is unresolved. Never share your wallet recovery phrase, private key, PIN, or seed words. This page does not verify blockchain payment or grant a download. If Trust Wallet returned an error and its activity shows no transfer, select the wallet-return issue below; do not invent a transaction ID or send funds again.

Before contacting support

  • Check your original invoice and the browser used for checkout.
  • In Trust Wallet, open TRX, find the completed transfer in activity/history, and copy its transaction ID/hash from the transaction details. App menus can vary.
  • Confirm the TRON network, recipient and amount against the original invoice. Never guess a destination address. For independent public-chain lookup, visit TRONSCAN manually; a chain record does not prove merchant entitlement.
  • Do not send TRX on another network, and do not disclose your wallet recovery phrase.
  • Use the official MojPlayWin support page when you have an internet connection.

Prepare your request

All information stays in this browser tab until you copy or download the message. Nothing is sent or saved automatically.

Do not enter card numbers, passwords, or wallet secrets. A transaction ID is public and is not a wallet secret. This tool only checks the format, not whether a payment occurred.

Your support message

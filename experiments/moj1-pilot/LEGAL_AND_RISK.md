# Moj 1 legal and risk gate checklist — 21 October 2026

**Preliminary only.** No legal opinion, license, securities classification or country approval has been obtained. Public trading is a goal, not an achieved or guaranteed outcome.

1. **Issuer and beneficial ownership:** Confirm legal issuer, contracting entity, token-holder rights, IP ownership, treasury controllers, and non-misleading publicity. Issuer and IP owner can be distinct.
2. **EU MiCA:** Classify utility vs asset-reference/e-money token vs financial instrument. Check white-paper, notification, marketing, CASP, custody, listing, market-abuse, consumer protection and relevant national authority requirements. https://www.esma.europa.eu/esmas-activities/digital-finance-and-innovation/markets-crypto-assets-regulation-mica
3. **United States:** Obtain counsel review of federal and state securities, money transmission/AML, sanctions, tax, promotions, rewards, gaming, privacy and age rules. Token naming alone does not resolve classification.
4. **Other markets and Iran:** Country-by-country restrictions for issuer, customer eligibility, exchanges, payments, sanctions and gaming; no circumvention or false residence claims.
5. **Google Play:** Games earning tokenized assets require the applicable Financial features declarations; do not promote expected earnings or use NFTs as wagers outside a licensed gambling regime. https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13607354
6. **Google rewarded advertising:** Do not offer cash, crypto or gift cards through rewarded Google ads. https://support.google.com/admob/answer/7313578
7. **Prizes:** Token rewards convertible into money can trigger real-money gaming/gambling obligations, even if players receive the tokens rather than cash. Require jurisdictional and age controls, approved prize funding, clear contest rules, and legal review before any prize redemption or betting features. https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9877032
8. **Security:** Reproducible builds, verified bytecode, independent audit, vault vesting, multisig, anti-phishing, contract access reviews, liquidity/treasury approvals, recovery plan.
9. **Disclosure:** No promise of price, appreciation, liquidity, guaranteed redeemability, exchange listing, fixed profit, or risk-free earnings. Disclose all allocations and insider release schedules.
10. **Mainnet gate:** Mainnet is blocked until all applicable compliance, contract safety, funding, owner-signing and publication approvals are documented. The pilot's single draft team vault is not equivalent to fully locked allocations.

The current code deliberately lacks a Mainnet deployment workflow and does not implement a token sale, DEX pool, custody or payment exchange.

About MojPlayWin

Original ideas. Research-led technology. Games, AI, software, learning and digital products designed to create practical value.

MojPlayWin (also written Moj Play Win) is an international-facing digital brand founded by Mojtaba Roozegar. We bring together creative technology, responsible innovation, independent research, interactive entertainment and accessible knowledge.

01 / IDENTITY

A digital brand built around ideas worth developing.

MojPlayWin connects several disciplines that increasingly shape how people learn, create, work and play. Our focus includes original games and interactive experiences, artificial intelligence, professional software and websites, digital commerce, research-based publications and technology education.

The brand grew from a practical belief: a promising idea should be examined, designed, tested and made useful rather than described as a finished success before people can actually use it. For that reason, we keep a visible distinction between educational information, concepts in development, prototypes, completed work and products or services currently available to customers.

Original by default. We value distinctive gameplay, independent creative work, clear authorship and meaningful usefulness. Rather than rely on reskins, unverified release claims or exaggerated promises, our direction is to build an original portfolio that earns trust through quality and working results.

06 / QUALITY & TRUST

Credibility is part of the product.

We aim to publish useful, original material and make material claims traceable. We distinguish a concept from a working prototype, a product listing from a verified purchase flow, and a planned collaboration from an executed agreement.

  • Originality and attribution: clear credit for authors, artists, collaborators and source material, with respect for intellectual property.
  • Research integrity: relevant sources, dates, methods and limitations for significant factual or scientific claims.
  • Responsible AI: human oversight where needed, safeguards against misleading outputs, and attention to confidentiality and privacy.
  • Customer clarity: actual availability, price, license, delivery, payment terms and support must be clear before a purchase is offered.
  • Security and inclusion: sensible protection of user information, accessible interfaces, maintainable systems and straightforward corrections when errors occur.
  • Regulated topics: education about finance, crypto or technical systems does not itself constitute brokerage, investment advice or an active payment service.

Our standard is simple: usefulness, reproducibility and transparency matter more than an impressive but unverified claim.

07 / FOUNDER & LEADERSHIP

Mojtaba Roozegar

Founder & CEO of MojPlayWin · Researcher · Business executive

Mojtaba Roozegar (مجتبی روزگار) is an Iranian entrepreneur and researcher based in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. He founded MojPlayWin to connect scientific thinking, creative digital work, entrepreneurship and new opportunities in technology and education.

His academic background has been described as a PhD in Agricultural Economics, specializing in Production Economics and Farm Management at Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM). His professional activities connect applied economic analysis, research communication and management with the design of intelligent industrial and commercial websites.

He is also the founder and CEO of Zomorod Melal Agro-Industry Company, a separate organization with a research and agricultural-industry focus. His wider professional experience and interests span scientific projects, agricultural business, jewelry and precious metals, handwoven carpets, hospitality, specialist digital services and international collaboration.

MojPlayWin's public profile emphasizes research-led technology, original games, software, artificial intelligence, digital publishing and education. A personal website separately presents his biography, experiences, essays, photographs and collaboration interests; it is not a substitute for this brand's company page.

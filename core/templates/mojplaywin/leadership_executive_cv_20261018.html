01 / ACADEMIC EDUCATION

Doctoral education in Agricultural Economics

Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) — Agricultural Economics

University reported: Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM), Malaysia.

Specialization: Production Economics and Farm Management.

Interdisciplinary applications: agricultural decision theory, production-resource economics, cost and revenue structures, farm-level management, enterprise performance, risk assessment and research methods.

Production economics studies the relationship between scarce resources and measurable outputs. In agriculture, this includes land use, irrigation water, labor, capital, machinery, animal or crop inputs, yields, costs and the timing of commercial decisions. It provides analytical methods for examining how farms and agro-industrial enterprises can make defensible choices under imperfect information and uncertainty.

Farm management connects these economic concepts to actual production systems. It considers production planning, input purchasing, whole-enterprise budgeting, cash-flow timing, risk diversification, labor allocation and the evaluation of alternative operating scenarios. Scientific interpretation requires comparison of assumptions and accounting for environmental and commercial constraints.

Moj Prop by MojPlayWin

Information inquiry privacy notice

Scope: Only the optional information-request form at /moj-prop/interest/. This form is not a funded account application, financial advice, automated trading service, KYC process, or customer payment.

What is collected

The email address you voluntarily submit, a keyed pseudonymous duplicate-detection digest, the version of your explicit consent, creation time, and a retention deadline. A short-lived, non-reversible request rate-limit bucket is derived from network metadata; the raw IP address is not stored in the inquiry record.

Purpose and handling

Your contact information is held solely to process your request for factual program information. No brokerage, payment, or trading entitlement arises. This form does not itself send you an email, enroll you into marketing, or transfer an account to a broker. Access to stored requests is restricted to authorized staff.

Retention

Inquiries are marked with a maximum 30-day retention deadline. A protected operational cleanup procedure must delete expired information. Until that procedure is approved and operational, this intake remains disabled.

Questions and privacy requests

For access, correction or deletion requests, use the official MojPlayWin support contact support@mojplaywin.com. Before enabling the form, the operator must verify that the privacy notice, jurisdiction requirements, response process, and retention cleanup are legally sufficient.

Founder profile · Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada

Mojtaba Roozegar

Researcher · Chief Executive Officer · Editor-in-Chief · Website Designer & Developer

Mojtaba Roozegar is based in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. His work spans agricultural economics, production economics, farm management, scientific research, technology, software development and digital entrepreneurship.

His professional interests also include education, publishing, digital platforms, and interdisciplinary research. He is the designer and developer of MojPlayWin.

Areas of expertise and interest

Agricultural economics and farm management; research and educational publishing; artificial intelligence and software; business development; digital media and content; precious metals and gemstones; traditional carpets and textiles; culinary arts; and specialist animal breeding.

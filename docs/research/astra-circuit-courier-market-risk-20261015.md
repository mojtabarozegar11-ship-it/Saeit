## What is actually different, only as a testable hypothesis

This *original implementation* combines three ordered-or-unordered objective
pickups, exact shortest-route hints, a hard step budget, 36 stable
algorithmically generated layouts, and a local no-network progression log.
These are combinable common puzzle mechanics, **not proven novelty**.
No direct competitor feature matrix has yet demonstrated a moat.

## Proposed review criteria (not claimed achieved)

1. **Gameplay usability:** at least 8 consented independent target-player
   sessions on mobile and desktop; verify that they understand controls,
   recover from losses, and complete a beginner level without intervention.
2. **Accessibility:** independent keyboard, touch, contrast and screen reader
   testing. DOM roles alone are not sufficient.
3. **Difficulty curve:** collect completion rates and time/move ratios across
   early, middle and late stages; require human sign-off on procedurally
   produced layouts instead of trusting pure BFS solvability.
4. **Originality:** review puzzle mechanic, icon asset and brand rights
   against actual competing games. No copied level or artwork may ship.
5. **Willingness to pay:** use consented user interviews and tested pricing
   proposals. Free browser competitors make paid sales *more* uncertain.
6. **Security and release:** independent distribution build, CSP, local data,
   permissions, store terms, payment and license/delivery QA. None passed yet.

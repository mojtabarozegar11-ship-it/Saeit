## Source-based product choice, R&D only

**Research priority 1 — Narrow farm procurement quote and substitution audit:** build an interpretable side-by-side comparison with standardized quote units, freight, lead time, quality specification, contract terms and tax treatment *only where sourced from actual user quotes*. Expose missing data, reason codes and a reproducible calculation trail. This potentially removes clerical errors more directly than a generic farm budget. The buyer/workflow problem, savings, time reduction and price acceptance **have not yet been evidenced**.

**Research priority 2 — Orchard investment and lifecycle comparison:** use multi-year cash flow, operating versus establishment cost, NPV sensitivity, seasonal risks and source traceability. Benchmark university orchard cost and land-water tools before presenting any unique value claim. Demand and paid pricing remain unknown.

**Lower priority — General land/water margin optimizer:** an existing UC Davis planning tool includes agronomic rotation and multi-year water trade-offs beyond the Astra demonstration's simplified two-resource solver. Develop only against a distinct user scenario with independently checked agronomy, water rights and expert domain feedback.

## Requirements before claiming a qualified retail product

1. **Real sourcing:** for each candidate capture exact source revision, author/publisher, date, retained user rights, retrieval proof, nonduplicated independent evidence and documented limitations. The aspiration of 100 independent evidence items per product **is not met by retrieving five web pages**.
2. **Domain review:** independent methods and sensitivity validation against reference examples, unexpected edge cases, soil, rotation, regional applicability, financial calculation rules and reproducibility.
3. **Customer interviews:** actual interested buyers, problem interviews, willingness-to-pay evidence, competing free products and honest support requirements. Avoid fake reviews, a fabricated base price or artificial 90% discounts.
4. **Commercial releases:** before automatic storefront delivery, independently signed approval of artifact rights, quality, accessibility, refund/help policy, support burden, exact license/download behavior and fully reconciled payment. No automatic approval is derived from a URL being online.
5. **Quality metrics:** no ungrounded 100/100 rating. Start with objective acceptance tests and explicit unresolved blockers; have qualified reviewers agree to scoring rubric when evidence exists.

# Astra V2 market gap and commercialization experiments — 2026-10-15

**Scope:** research-only competitive analysis for three existing private, QA-tested Astra builders. No new customer purchases, user interviews, payment evidence, legal opinion, independent economist signoff or genuine buyer demand is claimed. Publicly available product features are not proof of the corresponding current product's adoption. URLs checked 2026-10-15; pages may change.

## Experiment queue, not product inventory

1. **E-ORCHARD**: Ask independent orchard managers to solve identical anonymized cash-flow comparisons with existing workbook and Astra. Capture consent, elapsed time, audit error count, usability and willingness to pay. Do not edit the result or manufacture reviewers.
2. **E-PROCUREMENT**: With explicit permission, test anonymized real vendor quotes and shipping/tax/minimum-order cases against a manual workflow. Require exact agreement and independently measure any decisions improved or errors flagged. Validate whether the workflow itself merits paid purchase.
3. **E-WATER**: Technical agricultural economist independently checks unit dimensions, crop constraints, feasibility, drought sensitivity and solver optimality against small provable models. Then run a feature-by-feature comparison with UC Davis/K-State and document where Astra is **weaker**, equivalent or meaningfully different.
4. **E-COMMERCE**: For any proposed retail release, separately establish distribution legitimacy, rights/licenses, buyer eligibility, accessible documentation, maintenance burden, fair pricing evidence and a real end-to-end checkout/settlement test. No test receipts as revenue.

## Decision gates

- **Technical integrity**: machine-verifiable source, ZIP, immutable QA manifest and accepted private queue row. Automated by scripts/astra_v2_release_gate.py; success does **not** imply buyer value.
- **Domain expert validation**: missing for all three. Cannot be inferred from successful Decimal/math unit tests.
- **Demand validation**: missing for all three. Competitor pages and academic resources demonstrate alternatives, not customer willingness to pay.
- **Distribution and price**: not independently verified. Do not invent original/list price and then apply a 90% discount; a transparent reduction is meaningful only against an honestly established price.
- **Release**: zero, until all separate gate owners supply inspectable evidence. A 90+/100 commercial score remains **unset**, not zero or 100 by fiat.

## Product selection implication

Prioritize research on a supplier quote audit and orchard comparison for narrower, testable decision problems. Deprioritize the generalized water planner as a commercial product while no measurable advantage over sophisticated university tools has been demonstrated. This is a **research prioritization hypothesis**, not a measured market ranking.

## No-order automation boundaries

Private factory may continually generate QA-tested original source revisions and bounded fictional course exercises without orders. Neither source-revision hashes nor hourly exercise ZIPs count as new paid products. A development research backlog can be created from these experiments; automatically promoting the backlog to published goods before external evidence is prohibited.

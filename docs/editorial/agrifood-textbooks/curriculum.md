## Dependency and assessment standard

Each chapter depends on preceding conceptual prerequisites, from foundations through theory and quantitative methods to applied integration. A chapter's learning outcomes are to formulate the relevant economic decision problem, derive or estimate its implications, interpret uncertainty and welfare, and critique an empirical case. Assessment includes a mathematical derivation, a reproducible exercise, a source audit, and an applied policy or management decision. Chapter-level outlines and detailed prerequisites are in the separate editorial working package, not yet part of this public repository.

## Production pipeline

For each hourly run: plan n+2; prepare n+1; develop n; check n using evidence; update checkpoint and prepare publication. Failed chapters stay open. Neither the passage of an hour nor the presence of 5,000 words is sufficient to certify a chapter.

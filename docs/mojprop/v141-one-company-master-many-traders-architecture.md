# MOJ PROP v1.4.1 — One COMPANY master account, many website traders

**Owner-confirmed operating model:** traders use the MOJ PROP **website trading panel**, not their own LiteFinance accounts. The WEBSITE BACKEND connects to **one company-owned LiteFinance master account**. Traders have NO individual LiteFinance registrations, direct broker logins, account passwords, company-account access or third-party deposits. All broker funding belongs to the company. Separately, a customer may pay 30 native TRX to the MOJ PROP seller **only after lawful enrollment and approved settlement**; that is not a deposit to LiteFinance.

## Correct separation of operations

1. **Customer authentication and virtual portfolio:** one `trader_ref`, one or more `challenge_ref`, individual virtual equity/positions, consent and product contract. Tenant IDs and records require strict server-side isolation. An order cannot be tagged with another trader's challenge ID.
2. **Two-stage evaluation / DEMO:** customers' site transactions are virtual and scored with their own signed, independently recorded event streams. **DEMO trades do not go to the real company broker account**. Each challenge has its own 28-day window, at least six days per stage, 50 unique closed virtual positions total and its own daily/overall drawdown. 100 users can each have separate demo books; one real account does NOT imply each has $100 real money behind it.
3. **Company master execution — only if separately authorized:** a trusted backend can eventually review approved consolidated order intents. The account and capital belong solely to the company, and only a server-side service holds approved integration credentials, with short-lived restricted access and no browser exposure. All orders go through company aggregate risk limits, broker-specific lot/minimum-margin/leverage, queue/reservation, signed acknowledgements and deduplication.
4. **Internal risk vs broker netting:** at a Netting MT5 account there can be only one position per symbol even with many opposing user intents. At Hedging accounts there can be several positions but margin constraints differ; this setting is determined by the broker. Thus each user's virtual portfolio and P&L must remain separate from the net broker position. The backend must maintain an authoritative per-user double-entry event ledger and independent allocation / fills/fees/slippage rules, not count shared broker positions as individual closed customer trades.
5. **Capital mathematics:** proposed Plan1 company master equity USD 100, max nominal risk-at-stop USD 3 per new intended order and USD 8 aggregate including existing and reserved risk. This means 100 clients each simultaneously demanding USD 3 risk would request **USD 300 aggregate stop risk**, exceeding the USD 8 company cap even before margin/slippage. A single account is **technically one account** but not unlimited capital or unlimited concurrent real execution. Therefore the website evaluation should remain demo unless a separate lawful company capital/risk plan supports selected live flows.
6. **Broker/legal approval:** no outside trader depositing money is contemplated. AML third-party-deposit restrictions are NOT this model's funding barrier. However routing order decisions from many outside website users to a single company broker account may fall under the broker's account-control, managed-account, introducing/brokerage and applicable financial-regulatory terms. It is **not independently proven permitted**. Prior LiteFinance email is a pending inquiry, not permission. The precise question is whether this backend order origination is allowed, not whether each user can pay or log in.
7. **Payouts and marketing:** fee receipts, simulated demo gains, company broker account profit, and legally distributable trader payouts are FOUR separate ledgers. Customer payment never becomes broker equity automatically. Company 70/30 proposed realized profit split is not automatically distributable until verified source statements and applicable obligations. Any requirement to publish financial promotion needs applicable independent legal review.

## Current implementation truth — DO NOT MISSTATE

- `prop_firm/plan1_v141_simulated_challenge_engine.py`, the HMAC signed journal intake and isolated `PropV141SimState` records are **pure/DB-proposed demo mathematics**, not an authenticated customer trading terminal or verified live LiteFinance adapter.
- `prop_firm/plan1_v141_shared_company_master_review.py` is the first independent **pure mathematical capacity model** for this specific one-master-account design. It accepts distinct virtual traders/challenges, protects challenge ownership, rejects replayed order refs, accounts for pending/old company risk, checks limited margin and blocks opposing Netting intents absent internal reconciliation. All `live_order_routing_authorized`, `broker_multitenant_routing_contractually_authorized`, `paid_enrollment_authorized` and `payout_authorized` flags remain **FALSE**.
- **Legacy v1.2 `prop_firm/allocation_service.py` explicitly rejects one funded broker reference backing multiple individual accounts. Do NOT remove that invariant blindly**; it prevents accidentally crediting the same brokerage profit several times. The new version needs a separate company master-position ledger and per-user attribution system before a change to live operation.
- The owner reports that the website system is connected to LiteFinance. **No independently authenticated live connector state, order receipt, broker-authorized API account type or current account holdings have been verified in this review.** Treat claimed connectivity as owner-supplied architecture, not completion evidence.
- No new broker accounts or deposits, no website production deployment, no real trades, no money movement, and no live payout were created.

## Planned authenticated production interfaces (NOT YET IMPLEMENTED)

- `POST /internal/challenges/{opaque-ref}/demo-orders` — server-owned KYC/contract scope; per-user virtual balance with atomic order idempotency; signed quote timestamps and commission; append-only audit; 4%/8% stage risk enforced. No broker call.
- `POST /internal/company-master/order-intents` — separately licensed/approved operator; authenticated exact account id and one-use nonce; atomic aggregate risk reservation on company account; typed broker hedging/netting mode; no user-supplied broker credentials. Never expose externally before broker/legal confirmation.
- `GET /internal/challenges/{opaque-ref}/portfolio` — tenant scoped; source-of-truth virtual fills with immutable audit, never company master account credentials or other users' data.
- Independent broker reconciler and signed archival process must map all company executions and fees to **one broker master ledger** and separately map approved allocation decisions to customer statements. A successful client HTTP result or a simulated fill is NOT proof of actual brokerage execution.

## Real operational blockers
No verified lawful order routing for many users into one company account, no independently proven backend LiteFinance execution/permission, no authoritative tenant-safe virtual order server, no persistent per-trader double-entry ledger and shared broker allocation, no real MariaDB schema rehearsal/restore, no verified 30 TRX lawful checkout, no production full-site backup, no confirmed contract/customer eligibility, no independent funding/actual company capital. Therefore **NO-GO on live trades, paid sales, or payout**.

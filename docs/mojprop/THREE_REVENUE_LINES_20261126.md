# Moj Prop three revenue lines

Date: 2026-11-26

Moj Prop is commercially organized around three lines only:

1. **Prop** — Starter30 simulated challenge and future paid challenge participation after all release, payment, legal and settlement gates pass.
2. **Signals** — Mojtaba.bot.ai Signal Desk, beginning with educational scenarios and a future paid signal service; no guaranteed return or automatic trading claim.
3. **Education & Reports** — the $9.90 one-time Educational Weekly Brief, private market reports, risk checklists and other defined research deliverables.

## Acceptance rule

A line may be called commercially live only when its exact deliverable, payment receipt, fulfillment and support/recovery evidence are all verified. A visible CTA alone is not revenue evidence.

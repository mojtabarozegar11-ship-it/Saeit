1. Bank of Iran crypto broker rule dated 1404/07/13, article 38: **a licensed crypto broker may not use any crypto asset as a payment instrument**. Its wording has no 'Iranian buyers only' exception.
2. A type-2 crypto-broker authorization covers activities in article 2, subject to article 3 and its notes. **It does not authorize unrelated prop-firm challenge sales or TRX payment acceptance**. Article 1 defines the licensee as a legal person; the owner's claim of a type-2 permit must be checked against exact legal holder and granted activities. Do not transfer a company permit to personal merchant operations.
3. That broker-specific article does **not by itself establish a blanket prohibition or a blanket authorization** for a separately operated and actually independent natural person's export of a digital assessment service paid in TRX. Actual legal characterization, sanctions, foreign-exchange and tax treatment are unresolved. An independent arrangement must be substantive and declared; no straw company, false residence or bank/processor KYC circumvention.
4. US 31 CFR 560.201 generally forbids import into the US of Iranian-origin services unless licensed or exempt. 31 CFR 560.306 includes services performed by persons residing in Iran in definition of Iranian-origin services. Country of residence alone does not rule out US citizenship/ownership, US person or US payment nexus. OFAC rules may affect other participants and service providers as well. EXCLUDE US buyers/persons and US intermediaries unless separately counsel-cleared, including actual licenses/exemptions where applicable.
5. Paid nationwide recruitment/placement to employers in exchange for fees from jobseekers is restricted by Iran's 1401/11/12 employment-agency regulation Art 7 note 2. A genuinely separate independent examination with deliverables and optional free application route is a possible commercial structure, NOT a court/regulator ruling.
6. Consumer rights under Iranian Electronic Commerce Law (notably Arts 33–39) and applicable law of buyer jurisdiction: disclose merchant identity, service, evaluation deliverables and terms, refund/withdrawal treatment, evidence retention. No invented waiver of statutory rights.
7. Contracted traders sending suggestions or trading the owner's account are subject to broker client agreement, permitted automation/delegation and their employment/legal relationship. Never claim that an internet-facing trading evaluation produces an irrevocable obligation to fund a person.

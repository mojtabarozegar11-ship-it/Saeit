# Moj Prop manual-first commercial route

Date: 2026-11-26  
Source baseline: `4ccad180cc6ecd045c92ccbd117421c4fd2b167e`

## Purpose

This is the fallback route for MojProp.top while the automated Starter30/TRON release gate
is closed. It is a real sales-intake path, not a payment bypass.

The public proposition is:

> Request a written Moj Prop review and commercial offer.
> The review is manual. It is not a live trading account, not a funded account, and does
> not accept customer capital. No payment is taken by the interest form.

A lead is not a sale. A sale is recorded only after all of the following have been verified by
the owner:

1. the exact service and price were sent in writing;
2. the customer accepted that written offer;
3. an owner-approved payment channel was used;
4. payment was independently verified;
5. the promised written service was delivered and its delivery evidence was saved.

## Customer-safe offer

The first manual offer is a written, educational risk review for a prospective Moj Prop
participant. It may contain:

- a plain-language explanation of the simulation model;
- the current rules and risk limits;
- a personal pre-entry checklist;
- a written answer to the customer's questions;
- a clear statement of what is and is not available.

It must not contain:

- a promise of profit or a guaranteed reward;
- a claim that a funded account is already available;
- a request for a wallet key or customer capital;
- a payment instruction without a unique, owner-approved invoice;
- a claim that a lead, quote, or form submission is a completed purchase.

## Operating sequence

1. Attract qualified visitors with the English Moj Prop page and factual search content.
2. Send visitors to the existing contact-only CTA.
3. Collect only the contact details and consent already required by the form.
4. Create a lead reference; do not create an invoice.
5. Review the request manually.
6. Reject spam, unsupported jurisdictions, and requests requiring an unavailable feature.
7. Send a written offer draft for owner review.
8. Record the exact service, price, currency, delivery time, and limitations.
9. Obtain written customer acceptance.
10. Verify that an approved payment receiver is available before asking for payment.
11. Issue a unique invoice only through an approved channel.
12. Verify the transaction independently; never rely on a screenshot alone.
13. Deliver the written educational service.
14. Save a delivery reference and timestamp.
15. Mark revenue only after payment and delivery evidence exist.
16. Ask for a factual testimonial only after delivery.
17. Publish anonymised evidence only with consent.
18. Measure visitor-to-lead, lead-to-offer, offer-to-acceptance, and paid-delivery rates.
19. Keep Starter30 admission, recurring signals, and automatic rewards closed until their
    own release evidence gates are open.
20. When the isolated restore, rollback, and tenant-separation evidence is complete, run a
    separate release review; do not silently convert manual leads into paid enrollments.

## Release boundary

Starter30, deploy backend code, send email, or mutate production data. It is intentionally
compatible with the current fail-closed release gate.

The automated route remains:

`PRIVATE_RESTORE_EVIDENCE_DIR=absent`  
`RELEASE_GATE_OPEN=false`

That is a technical release blocker, not a reason to call manual leads paid customers.

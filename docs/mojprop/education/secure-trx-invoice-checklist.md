# A TRX invoice safety checklist for digital services

> **Moj Prop educational resource** · Simulated trading examples only. This is not investment advice, a guarantee of profit, a challenge pass, or a reward.

Crypto transfers are generally difficult to reverse. Before buying a digital service with native TRX, check what is being sold, the exact amount, the recipient, the network and refund conditions. Do not copy a receiving address from a social post or unsolicited message.

## Check the invoice
A merchant-issued invoice should identify the service and order, specify the exact amount, recipient and expiry, and explain fees and what to do if the payment cannot be matched. A 30 TRX invoice refers to the native amount, not a token with a similar ticker. Network fees are separate if applicable. Open checkout from the merchant's verified domain.

## Understand confirmation
Broadcasting a transaction is not the same as confirmed settlement. The merchant should reconcile transaction identity, native amount, recipient, order association and finality under its published process. Do not treat a screenshot as conclusive evidence. Save the invoice reference and transaction identifier privately.

## Avoid duplicate payment and impersonation
If status is pending, use official support before paying again. Never send a second transfer simply because an unverified account claims the first failed. Never share seed phrases, private keys or one-time codes. Check refund terms and avoid messages promising guaranteed investment returns.

## Review the service before buying
A simulated trading service does not imply a real brokerage account, ownership of virtual balances or guaranteed profit. Review its current eligibility, cancellation, delivery and reward terms.

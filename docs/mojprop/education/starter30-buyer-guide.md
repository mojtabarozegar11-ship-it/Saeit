## Frequently asked questions

### 1. Does paying 30 TRX mean I receive $1,000 in cash?

No. The $1,000 is a virtual reference balance for a trading simulation. It is not a bank deposit, loan, cash payout, personal investment account, or transferable customer property.

### 2. Is this a funded real-market account?

No. The public Starter 30 description says both the challenge and account stages are simulated and do not create a real brokerage account. Simulator fills and transaction costs may not reproduce live-market conditions.

### 3. How does the 6% daily limit interact with the 3% trade limit?

They measure different things. If the reference value were exactly $1,000, 6% would be $60 and 3% would be $30 as simple arithmetic examples. The actual evaluation may define daily loss using starting balance, equity, timestamps and resets; consult the current rulebook and checkout terms. **Two positions each sized at the full 3% limit could also violate a separate 4% aggregate open-risk limit.** Do not assume individually compliant trades are compliant in combination.

### 4. What does the 50% best-day consistency condition mean?

An illustrative $60 simulated net profit and $30 best day gives a 50% best-day-to-net-profit ratio. A further simulated loss could make that percentage higher even without another winning day. The operator's evaluation definitions and all other conditions control pass eligibility. A simple ratio is not a guarantee of passing or payment.

### 5. Is there a guaranteed rate of return?

No. There is no guaranteed trading result, successful evaluation, or amount of earned profit. The official product page describes an obligation to pay 100% of *qualifying profit earned* after passing and verification under its published terms; that should not be read as a prediction of profitable trading or proof of an independently observed reward transfer.

### 6. Where should I send TRX?

**Never to an address supplied by a social-media message, chat, screenshot or unverified support account.** Review the official product page and follow its secure checkout only if the service is available and the server issues an invoice with the exact recipient, amount and expiry. Do not send any transfer before that happens. This guide does not provide a receiving wallet address.

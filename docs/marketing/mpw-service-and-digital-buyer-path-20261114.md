## What to include in a service inquiry

Provide the business goal, website URL (if public), target customer, desired deliverable, available budget band, and a useful timeline. Avoid attaching credentials or personal financial records. You will need a written scope and price before approving delivery. For an unlisted service, use [the general inquiry](https://mojplaywin.com/mpw-editorial/revenue/inquiry.php?service=general).

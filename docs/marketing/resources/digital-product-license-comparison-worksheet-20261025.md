# Digital-product license comparison worksheet
Free buyer education resource · MojPlayWin · 25 October 2026

Digital products can differ materially in **usage rights**, even when two download files look similar. Use this checklist before purchase. It is not a license grant, legal opinion, or claim about any specific current product.

| Question | What to record | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Seller identity | Legal name, official support channel | Establish the contracting party |
| License type | Personal, commercial, seat-based or custom | Determine permitted use |
| Number of users | Named users, devices or seats | Avoid unauthorized redistribution |
| Modification | Whether edits and derivative works are allowed | Essential for design templates |
| Client delivery | Can a freelancer transfer a finished work? | Distinguish output from source resale |
| Source files | Which file types are included? | Avoid assuming editable source is supplied |
| Updates | Included, paid, or discretionary? | Budget for maintenance |
| Third-party assets | Fonts, photos, libraries and their licenses | Check downstream obligations |
| Support | Channel, hours and scope | Know what assistance is actually promised |
| Refunds | Applicable written terms and jurisdiction | Avoid assuming digital refunds |
| Privacy | Data required to fulfill purchase | Minimize unnecessary disclosure |
| Proof | Receipt, version, license text and checksum | Preserve evidence of entitlement |

## Decision workflow
1. Read the current official product description and license before paying; archive a dated copy.
2. Mark each criterion **confirmed**, **not stated**, or **not applicable**. Never interpret silence as permission.
3. For client projects, verify whether commercial work, redistribution, sublicensing and use of source assets are expressly permitted.
4. Ask the seller for clarification through the official support route if any material right is unclear.
5. Retain order ID, license version and correspondence. Do not post personal or payment details publicly.

## Example (illustrative, not a product promise)
A website template may permit building one customer website but prohibit selling the untouched template files. A digital book typically grants reading access, not redistribution. These examples are possibilities, not statements of MojPlayWin's actual licenses.

**Buyer decision:** proceed only when intended use is explicitly covered; otherwise seek written clarification or choose another product.

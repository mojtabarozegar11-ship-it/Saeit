Six printable storytime cards

Original prompts for shared reading, drawing and imaginative play. They work with many picture books; no text or illustrations from any commercial book are reproduced.

CARD 01 · BEFORE READING

Look, predict, wonder

What do you think might happen in this story?

Invite a guess from the title or cover. Ask what clue inspired the guess. There is no single right prediction.

Adaptation: let the child point or draw.

CARD 02 · DURING READING

Notice the rhythm

Which sound or repeated pattern did you enjoy?

Pause at a natural moment. Invite a gentle clap or tap, without pressuring children to perform.

Adaptation: silent tapping or a visual rhythm works too.

CARD 03 · AFTER READING

Three-scene story map

Can you show a beginning, a middle and an ending?

Draw three boxes. Put one action in each. Retell the sequence in the child's own words.

Adaptation: use gestures or picture choices.

CARD 04 · IMAGINATIVE PLAY

Pretend bakery

What would you make for a friendly customer?

Use paper shapes or toys to practise asking, offering, waiting and thanking. No real cooking is required.

Safety: avoid small loose objects for children who may mouth them.

CARD 05 · CREATIVE THINKING

Invent a helper

Who could help the characters solve a problem?

Draw a new helper and describe one useful thing they can do. Welcome unexpected ideas.

Adaptation: dictate a sentence to an adult.

CARD 06 · REFLECTION

Choose the next story

What part would you like to hear again?

Note the child's favorite moment, question or drawing. Let curiosity guide the next session.

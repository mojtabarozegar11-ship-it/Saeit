
# Private sales operations protocol — ten offers, one bot

All 10 source-backed or explicitly proposed revenue models are cataloged in scripts/mojtaba_revenue_portfolio.py. These ten **are not ten live businesses**. They are review categories under the existing one-bot authority mojtaba.bot.ai.

## Qualifying an inquiry
Take minimal initial context through the existing domain contact route. Keep human identifiers outside source control. Convert only non-sensitive counts into a local intake record with consent flag, data availability and one work_package. The CLI prints an anonymized internal review packet; it does not submit or send a quote.

## Price control
The only indicative non-binding price hypotheses are USD 29–49 for a single site diagnosis and USD 79–149 for a bounded repair. No local farm tariff, game price, publication price, supply price, industrial quote, research contract price or educational course price is inferred. No quote is final before case-by-case written owner approval.

## Delivery control
For a report: verify data provenance, accounting periods, formulas, limitations, independent QC and recipient acknowledgement. For code: reproduce fault, bound a patch, prove test and rollback, get written deployment approval. For assets: verify IP license, checksum and paid-only entitlement. For supply: verify stock/capacity, lawful shipping/food compliance and terms.

## Cash rule
No expenditure, development subscription, bulk outreach, customer account access, secret storage, trade execution, deployment, merge, invoice or financial commitment without explicit case-by-case approval. Any expansion funding must be based on money **actually received**, not listing forecasts.

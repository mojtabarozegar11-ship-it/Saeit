## Diagnosis deliverable
1. Observed route/version and date, with screenshots purged of checkout/customer secrets
2. Browser/device/network context (no raw IP, token, cookies, customer identities)
3. Reproduction: preconditions → action → expected/observed result
4. Impact class: broken order, failed delivery, invalid receipt, misleading price, nonfunctional form, accessibility
5. Source or server evidence tagged as `PROVEN`, `UNVERIFIED`, or `BLOCKED`
6. Root-cause hypothesis versus confirmed root cause, with confidence and evidence path
7. Bounded proposed remediation; independently approved scope and tested rollback

## Real delivery gate
A real paid diagnostic must cite observed environment, timestamp, reproducible steps, redacted evidence and limitations; identify which hypotheses were disproved; provide one bounded next-step proposal. Do not claim a fix or improvement without before/after evidence, owner QC and secure customer access. Never ask for credentials via ordinary email.

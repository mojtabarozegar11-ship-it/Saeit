# Factory policy: stringent quality AND genuine buyer value (2026-11-07)

Owner decision: removing the synthetic "quality 1000" score **did not** relax
standards. A technically valid ZIP is not automatically useful, commercial,
saleable or authorized for public release. A profitable business outcome cannot
be inferred from a unit-test count, AI-generated copy or a GitHub success badge.

## Release decisions (separate, fail-closed)

1. **Private technical manufacture:** exact source and ZIP integrity, successful
   real non-skipped tests, safe extraction, independently repeatable operation.
   Manufacture has no authority to list a product or process money.
2. **Customer-value proof:** identify the actual buyer, painful decision, scope
   boundaries and measurable outcome. Demonstrate at least two distinct buyer
   workflows with content-addressed observed results. Compare honestly with
   two existing alternatives, including relevant free ones; record their real
   capabilities instead of inventing weaknesses. Confirm the offer's exact
   delivery, license, support and remediation/refund process.
3. **Commercial demand:** cite specific market evidence with its limitations.
   For a **paid** product, published market research alone is insufficient:
   require a documented actual buyer enquiry, feedback, or genuine purchase
   linked to an evidence file. An internal script must never fabricate any of
   these records. Research sources are read by the reviewer, not certified
   automatically by their URL syntax.
4. **Final independent review:** the designated reviewer examines all shipped
   bytes, buyer workflow proof, offer and competing products, privacy/security,
   content claims, rights and delivery. A v3 final review binds its decision to
   the SHA-256 of the exact ZIP, manufacturing manifest, and buyer-value dossier.
   Unresolved findings block release. The structural validator cannot verify
   buyer sincerity or market willingness to pay; those need real-world evidence.
5. **Owner/site authority:** independent exact-artifact distribution rights,
   live site's actual release gate, exact-version owner permission, and payment
   and fulfilment checks remain separately mandatory. No auto-publish, fees or
   new commitments arise from any technical quality approval.

Required private dossier location:

`quality-clearance/<recipe>/<exact-zip-sha256>/buyer-value-evidence.json`

`schema: saeit.factory.buyer-value-evidence.v1`. Every workflow proof is a
separate hashed, immutable-in-effect file in that dossier's `buyer-proof/`
directory. The final reviewer explicitly binds its `buyer_value_evidence_sha256`
to the dossier; a later alteration invalidates the review. Paid offers need a
non-research, documentary demand signal. This mechanism records verifiable
evidence; it does **not** itself claim a guaranteed market, income or sales.

## First product: Farm Enterprise Economics Toolkit, Edition 1.4

Observed from archived buyer ZIP and recipe README:

- Working offline browser/Python financial calculator, portfolio contribution,
  break-even, nine volume/price sensitivities per enterprise, optional land/water
  efficiency ratios, local exports. Technical tests and integrity evidence exist.
- Clearly excluded: cash-flow schedule, capital investment, depreciation,
  financing, taxes and full investment appraisal. These are real purchase/use
  limitations, not minor copy edits.
- Important free substitutes already cover enterprise budgeting, whole-farm
  analysis, partial budgeting and cash-flow concepts. See official, independently
  authored educational sources:
  - Penn State Extension, *Budgeting for Agricultural Decision Making*:
    https://extension.psu.edu/budgeting-for-agricultural-decision-making
  - University of Minnesota Extension, *Farm Finance*:
    https://extension.umn.edu/agriculture/farm-operations-and-systems/agricultural-business-management/farm-finance
- **Commercial judgment today: HOLD paid launch.** No genuine buyer demand
  receipt, distinct customer-success dossier, independently checked pricing,
  guaranteed support channel, exact owner clearance or confirmed storefront
  mapping was presented. None should be invented to change the status.
- **Next production enhancement:** build and verify a genuinely complete farm
  decision workflow for the intended buyer, including appropriate period cash
  flow and investment comparison (or make an accurately bounded niche offer),
  test it from an extracted customer package, establish true differentiation,
  obtain buyer feedback, then issue exact-version commercial review. A free
  branded utility may be considered only through the separately authorized
  free-distribution gate and its rights/quality requirements.

No numerical grade replaces these acceptance conditions. No existing private
archive is silently edited; old v2 review receipts remain historical but are
insufficient for new public commercial authorizations.

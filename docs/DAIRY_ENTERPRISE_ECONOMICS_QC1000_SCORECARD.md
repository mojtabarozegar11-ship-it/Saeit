## Scoring rules

- Score each criterion from 0 to 10 for the exact file hash and version. Record a link or reproducible evidence for every non-zero score.
- Sum the 100 criterion scores: maximum 1,000 points. Reliability reruns are reported separately and never add quality points.
- A missing artifact or missing independent review is unscored, not a pass. The release stays blocked until the scorecard is complete.
- Rights, safety, formula correctness, data integrity, and delivery criteria must each receive 10/10. Any critical score below 10 blocks release regardless of total.
- No criterion may be scored from an assertion alone. Evidence must identify the workbook cell/sheet, source, reproducible calculation, or reviewer record.

Protect five root lanes

Enemies move from right to left. Choose a plant below, then tap an empty garden tile to plant it. Defenders fire along their lane; mist plants slow invaders; wells restore water. Save water for an emergency rainburst. A pest reaching the root damages orchard health.

Keyboard: 1 = Thorn, 2 = Mist, 3 = Well; Space = rainburst; P = pause. All actions also have touch-friendly buttons. Hold position strategically; planting in the wrong lane wastes water. This is a fixed-scope tactical prototype, not a commercially accepted game.

Your browser must support Canvas 2D to play.

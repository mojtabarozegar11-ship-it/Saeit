WHAT YOU RECEIVE

From dossier to accountable findings.

Every assessment is version-specific. An inquiry is not an accepted contract. A case cannot be labelled “reviewed” without a staff reviewer recording evidence for all eight criteria and affirming that material conflicts are resolved.

DELIVERABLE A

Private case record

Status history, staff questions and owner replies, product metadata and SHA-256 fingerprints of submitted evidence files.

DELIVERABLE B

Documented findings

Evidence for each assessment dimension, transparent scoring, critical-gate outcomes and a downloadable private JSON report when completed.

DELIVERABLE C

Reviewer declaration

A named staff account, documented methodology, scope boundaries and independence statement.

IMPORTANT DISCLOSURE

Professional standards. Honest claims.

No manufactured experts

Software cannot create a real qualified independent reviewer. Human competence and independence must be established for each engagement.

No surprise charges

No payment is processed when you submit an inquiry. Any fee, deliverable or deadline requires a written offer and separate acceptance.

No automatic certification

A reviewer-attested report is not a government-issued license, ISO accreditation, security certification or formal third-party endorsement.

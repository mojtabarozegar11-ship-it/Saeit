OPEN METHODOLOGY / VERSION 2026.1

A transparent process.
An accountable judgment.

This document explains the calculation method, limitations and professional conditions for independent assessment at AYAR. Publishing a transparent methodology does not imply third-party accreditation or official certification.

01. Nature of the service

AYAR has two distinct pathways: a free, browser-only self-assessment desk, and a private dossier intake and tracking workflow. An applicant can submit a specific product, upload limited supporting evidence, receive a confidential case reference and view subsequent staff dispositions. Authorized operators can record a documented AYAR Expert Engine AI-assisted assessment after the applicant authorizes AI processing. The model is invoked separately through a controlled manual workflow, not through a continuously connected website API.

The self-assessment is based only on user-entered information. Registering a dossier does not perform security testing, establish ownership, reserve reviewer capacity or enter a paid contract. An AYAR AI-assisted result is not an accreditation certificate, quality guarantee, or proof of independent human verification. Independence must be established for each actual engagement, not inferred from software.

02. Weighted scoring

Each criterion requires an integer score from 0 to 5 and an evidence statement of at least 12 characters. Every product category has eight criteria with weights totaling exactly 100%. No overall score is displayed until all criteria and the product name are complete.

Weighted total = Σ (criterion score ÷ 5 × criterion weight). The result ranges from 0 to 100 and reflects only the input of the person completing the assessment.

ScorePreliminary labelInterpretation

Below 70Further review requiredSubstantial weaknesses in recorded scores

A critical criterion—such as rights ownership, security or functional correctness—scored below 3 overrides every positive overall grade with a critical issue warning.

03. Evidence record

  • For every finding, identify the tested version, method, observed outcome, date and documentary reference.
  • Distinguish scientific evidence from opinion, marketing claims and editorial interpretation.
  • Software security requires risk-appropriate checks of permissions, data handling, configuration, dependencies and vulnerability exposure.
  • Licensing and ownership documents must match the actual product and exact version under review.
  • State unavailable evidence, untested components and uncertainty explicitly.

04. Independence & conflicts

AYAR identifies itself as part of MojPlayWin and does not conceal its commercial affiliation. AYAR Expert Engine is an AI-assisted internal analytical process. A genuinely independent third-party opinion would require a separately qualified external reviewer, documented conflicts, evidence and challenge procedures.

Until these conditions are documented for an individual engagement, its output is a preliminary assessment. A logo, reference number or badge does not establish independence.

05. Reproducibility & appeals

Submitted cases now have a private chronological event log. Staff may record a disposition and an eight-criterion AI-assisted report only after a real model analysis session, evidence inspection, explicit applicant AI consent and provenance recording. Final results cannot be silently overwritten. An applicant may authorize an abbreviated public summary; absence of consent keeps the case private.

This is not an independent-certification registry or a repository of accredited certificates. Formal appeals, external professional accreditation, contractual terms and validity periods require further operational arrangements.

06. Privacy & report integrity

The preliminary self-assessment tool runs in your browser without submitting its input to AYAR. The separate dossier intake form deliberately sends your submitted information and optional attachments to private server storage; the two features have different data handling. Read the privacy and evidence policy before uploading. Downloaded preliminary JSON contains a SHA-256 checksum computed over the browser-entered input. It detects changed data but is not a digital signature or source authentication.

07. Professional review requirements

Private intake, tracking credentials, staff access control and structured reviewer records are implemented in the AYAR workflow. Commercial independent certification still depends on external professional qualification, contractual terms, pricing and a formal appeal procedure. The free manual AI workflow uses no paid model API and is not an external certification. The system does not invent real-world reviewers or claim that every application will be accepted.

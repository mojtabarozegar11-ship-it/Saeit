Find the light. Escape the maze.

Explore a shifting digital labyrinth whose walls rebuild each sector. Gather three scattered beacons, read the glow around you, and reach the exit while roaming sentinels patrol.

Free · No account · No purchases · Local-only records

Keyboard + touch supported

How to play

  • Use arrow keys, WASD, the directional buttons or tap an adjacent corridor.
  • Find all three golden beacons before entering the portal.
  • Avoid pink sentinels. Three collisions end your expedition.

About this beta

Beacon Labyrinth is an original, free-to-play browser prototype developed for player feedback in the United States and worldwide. It is not a Google Play release or an app-store certification. Best scores stay locally on your device; we don't ask for an account or payment to play.

Use the Pause button to take a break. The game pauses automatically when you switch away from this tab.

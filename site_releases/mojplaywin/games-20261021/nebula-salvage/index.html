Orbit, aim, and recover.

The cargo is trapped in unstable orbits. Fire energy probes from your salvage ship, bend their trajectories around a gravity well and recover drifting containers before the window closes.

Free · No account · No purchases · Local-only records

Keyboard + touch supported

How to play

  • Tap the arena to aim and launch a probe toward moving cargo.
  • Press Space or Fire for auto-aim, or use the left and right aim controls.
  • Collect five cargo crates in each of three sectors while conserving hull.

About this beta

Nebula Salvage is an original, free-to-play browser prototype developed for player feedback in the United States and worldwide. It is not a Google Play release or an app-store certification. Best scores stay locally on your device; we don't ask for an account or payment to play.

Use the Pause button to take a break. The game pauses automatically when you switch away from this tab.

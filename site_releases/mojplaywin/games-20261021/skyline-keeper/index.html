Defend the last illuminated city.

Your last city needs a defense plan. Place pulse, beam and frost turrets on eight platforms, earn energy for every interception and defeat ten escalating drone waves.

Free · No account · No purchases · Local-only records

Keyboard + touch supported

How to play

  • Select Pulse (1), Beam (2) or Frost (3), then tap an empty platform.
  • Keyboard: arrow keys select one of eight highlighted platforms; Enter or Space deploys or upgrades. Press 1–3 to select a turret.
  • Tap an occupied platform to upgrade a turret for 75 energy.
  • Prevent drones from reaching the city. Beat ten waves to win.

About this beta

Skyline Keeper is an original, free-to-play browser prototype developed for player feedback in the United States and worldwide. It is not a Google Play release or an app-store certification. Best scores stay locally on your device; we don't ask for an account or payment to play.

Use the Pause button to take a break. The game pauses automatically when you switch away from this tab.

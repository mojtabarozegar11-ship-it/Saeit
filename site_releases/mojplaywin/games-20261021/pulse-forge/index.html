A living reactor core. One moving spark. Eighteen escalating rings. Strike at exactly the right moment, protect your shields and keep your combo alive.

Free · No account · No purchases · Local-only records

Keyboard + touch supported

How to play

  • Tap the arena or press Space to strike when the blue needle meets the gold arc.
  • Use the Focus button or F key for a limited time-dilation assist.
  • Clear 18 reactor rings before losing three shields.

About this beta

Pulse Forge is an original, free-to-play browser prototype developed for player feedback in the United States and worldwide. It is not a Google Play release or an app-store certification. Best scores stay locally on your device; we don't ask for an account or payment to play.

Use the Pause button to take a break. The game pauses automatically when you switch away from this tab.

Watch. Remember. Bloom.

An impossible flower has six luminous petals and a growing secret pattern. Watch carefully, repeat the sequence and nurture your memory over fifteen increasingly complex rounds.

Free · No account · No purchases · Local-only records

Keyboard + touch supported

How to play

  • Observe the glowing petal sequence before making a move.
  • Tap petals or press the matching keys 1–6 in the same order.
  • Complete 15 rounds before running out of three chances.

About this beta

Cipher Garden is an original, free-to-play browser prototype developed for player feedback in the United States and worldwide. It is not a Google Play release or an app-store certification. Best scores stay locally on your device; we don't ask for an account or payment to play.

Use the Pause button to take a break. The game pauses automatically when you switch away from this tab.

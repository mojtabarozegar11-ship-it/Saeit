# Irrigation Field Notes: Root-Zone Water Storage and Trigger Thresholds

## 09. Calculate plant-available water in the root zone
**Problem.** A volumetric moisture reading alone does not tell a grower how much water the crop can still extract.

**Field procedure.** Obtain field capacity (FC) and permanent wilting point (PWP) for the soil layers within the effective root zone. Convert both to volumetric fractions. For a reasonably uniform soil layer, total available water in millimeters is (FC - PWP) multiplied by root depth in meters and by 1000. For multiple layers, calculate and sum each layer separately.

**Worked example.** FC = 0.30, PWP = 0.16 and root depth = 0.40 m yield (0.30 - 0.16) * 0.40 * 1000 = 56 mm. This is storage capacity, not today's irrigation recommendation.

**Limitations.** Root depth changes over the season. Salinity, hardpans, uneven wetting and soil variability may restrict effective uptake. Soil properties must be locally measured.

## 10. Determine a defensible irrigation trigger
**Problem.** Waiting until all available soil water is depleted can expose the crop to avoidable water stress.

**Field procedure.** Select an allowable depletion fraction based on crop, growth stage and evaporative demand. Multiply this fraction by the available water in the active root zone. Track cumulative crop evapotranspiration and effective rainfall against the measured starting moisture.

**Worked example.** If the available storage is 56 mm and a site-specific allowable depletion of 0.50 is justified, the trigger is 28 mm of depletion. A 4 mm/day net withdrawal would reach that level in approximately seven days from field capacity, assuming no other inputs or losses.

**Limitations.** The fraction 0.50 is illustrative, not universal. Rainfall, drainage, irrigation nonuniformity and changing root depth can invalidate the simple timeline.

**Reference:** FAO Irrigation and Drainage Paper 56 (Allen et al., 1998), https://www.fao.org/4/X0490E/X0490E00.htm . The examples are educational calculations, not measurements from a specific farm.

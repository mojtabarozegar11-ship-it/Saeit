## Conditional Instagram/Facebook compliance review

Official U.S. FTC guidance for covered endorsements explains that material promotional relationships/incentives must be disclosed **clearly and conspicuously**, and that an interface tool may be insufficient on its own. A required post as a condition of participation may create a relevant material connection. Source: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking

Meta branded content requires appropriate disclosures and platform policy review, and may require paid-partnership labeling and/or permissions under its terms; applicability must be reviewed for account type, jurisdiction and promotional arrangement. Source: https://www.facebook.com/business/help/ (the country/account-specific current policy must be independently checked before release).

**Jurisdiction caveat:** FTC is specifically U.S. guidance, not universal law. Do not claim that posting is automatically lawful in every country, or force access to any participant's personal accounts. Do not use the owner's personal Instagram or related Facebook. Any lawful alternative to a compulsory endorsement requires owner/legal approval of the contract **before** invoicing.

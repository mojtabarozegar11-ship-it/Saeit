A CLEAR WORKFLOW

Transparent collaboration,
from brief to delivery.

Each project starts with a conversation about the intended outcome. After reviewing the available files, we agree the scope, review points and delivery format before drafting begins.

✓1. Understand the brief
We review your requirements, dimensions, reference files and intended use.
✓2. Agree scope and estimate
Deliverables, review rounds and a proposed timeline are documented first.
✓3. Draft and review in stages
Milestone files let you check dimensions, coordination and presentation as work progresses.
✓4. Receive organized deliverables
Agreed file formats are delivered with clear naming and handover notes.
Drafting services do not imply statutory approval, professional sign-off or a licensed stamp. Where a regulated submission is required, eligibility and review by an appropriately qualified local professional must be arranged separately.

BEFORE WE BEGIN

Frequently asked questions

Share the source files and intended outcome when you request a quote for a more useful estimate.

Contact the studio ←

What do I need to provide to get started?

A short brief, available drawings or dimensions, the intended output and your target date. Send what you have, and we can identify any missing information.

How is pricing determined?

Pricing depends on scope, drawing complexity, turnaround and agreed review rounds. A written estimate is provided before work begins.

Which file formats can you deliver?

Formats are agreed for each project and may include PDF, editable drawing files and presentation images, depending on the work requested.

Can the drawings be stamped or approved?

Drafting and visualization do not constitute statutory approval. Any regulated submission must be reviewed and signed by a suitably qualified professional under local rules.

How can I pay?

Online checkout will follow payment-provider onboarding. Until the provider is configured and tested, this site only records project enquiries and never asks for card details.

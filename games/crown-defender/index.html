DEFEND THE CROWN

The Royal Nebula is under siege. Fight through enemy formations, defeat three-phase flagships and spend earned shards between waves to enhance your starfighter.

Shards are earned through play. All upgrades reset when you start a new mission. No real-world payments.

🎮 How to play

← ↑ → ↓ or W A S D move · Space shoot · P pause. On mobile, drag across the arena to steer or use the on-screen buttons. Auto-fire is enabled by default. Avoid crimson projectiles and defend your three hull points.

✦ Royal Nebula upgrades

Earn shards by destroying threats; every third wave, improve your weapons, hull or engines. Collect gold relics, energy shields, hull repairs, and triple or laser boosts. Every fourth wave unleashes a three-phase boss. Your high score stays only in this browser. No account or purchases.

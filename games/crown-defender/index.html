DEFEND THE CROWN

Defeat approaching drones, dodge enemy fire and collect power-ups. Move with WASD or arrows; hold Space to fire. On touchscreens, drag your ship or use the buttons.

🎮 How to play

← ↑ → ↓ or W A S D move · Space shoot · P pause. On mobile, drag across the arena to steer or use the on-screen buttons. Auto-fire is enabled by default. Avoid crimson projectiles and defend your three hull points.

🏆 Power-ups & progression

Collect ✦ gold relics for bonus score, ♢ cyan shields, ✚ green repairs, and ⚡ weapon boosts. Every wave becomes tougher, with an armored crown-breaker appearing every fourth wave. Your best score is saved only in this browser.

Mission Console

Each blue tile contains a rotating slash or backslash mirror. Yellow light travels along rows or columns and reflects when it hits a mirror. Reach the green receiver without escaping the grid.

Keyboard: Tab through mirror buttons and press Enter or Space to rotate. Every solved stage unlocks the next and saves locally; the game needs no login. Hint uses one of three charges per stage.

Scoring

Earn 3 stars for solving in few moves without hints, 2 for a longer solution and 1 after heavy assistance. Stars are feedback rather than paid currency.
